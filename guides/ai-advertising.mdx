"Agentic" advertising means an AI assistant reads your accounts, reasons about them and proposes work, instead of you clicking through each ads manager. With ADSMCP the split of responsibilities is fixed:

| Who | Does what |
| --- | --- |
| Your AI client and model (Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor, …) | Understands the request, chooses tools, writes the analysis and copy. You pay your AI provider for it. |
| ADSMCP | Holds the platform connections, returns account data and computed analysis, validates drafts, and stages changes. |
| You | Set targets and the spend cap, and approve or reject every change on the dashboard. |

## What an agent can do well today

- **Reporting:** pull performance from Google, Meta, Amazon, TikTok, LinkedIn and ChatGPT Ads and compare it with the targets in `STRATEGY.md` (`/adsmcp:performance-review`).
- **Waste checks:** rank spend that misses your targets (`/adsmcp:wasted-spend`).
- **Drafting:** validate campaign drafts and stage paused campaigns (`/adsmcp:campaign`).
- **Monitoring:** threshold [alerts](/alerts), [scheduled briefs](/reports) and [automation rules](/automation) that notify or stage changes.

## What stays with you

- Every change is staged as a pending action; nothing happens in the ad account until a person approves it on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page.
- New campaigns are created paused. Turning them on is a separate approval.
- Budget changes and enables are checked against your [spend cap](/safety#spend-cap).
- Deletes need `confirm_delete: true` and approval.
