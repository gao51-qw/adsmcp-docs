---
title: "Creatives"
description: "Browse creative performance and fatigue for Meta, Google, and TikTok without publishing an ad."
---

[Creatives](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/creatives) lists ad creatives already in connected accounts. It is a read screen. It does not upload a file or turn an ad on.

Filters:

- Platform: all, Meta, Google, or TikTok.
- Date range: last 7, 14, 30, or 90 days.
- Ad type: all, image, video, carousel, or collection.

The Performance tab shows delivery. The Fatigue tab shows fatigue signals for those same rows. LinkedIn and Amazon creatives are not in this filter list. Use the platform tools on [LinkedIn Ads](/ad-platforms/linkedin-ads) and [Amazon Ads](/ad-platforms/amazon-ads) for those reads.

Creating a new paused ad is a write on the platform page, then [Approvals](/approvals) if enabling it would spend.
