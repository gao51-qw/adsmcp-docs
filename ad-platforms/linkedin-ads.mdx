ADSMCP connects your AI client to LinkedIn Ads: ad accounts, organizations, campaign groups, campaigns, creatives, lead forms and analytics.

## Connect

Connect LinkedIn on [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections) (OAuth; re-authorize if ad-account scopes are missing). Then call `get_connections_status` and `get_linkedin_ad_accounts`. Pass the `account_id` as a string.

## What you can do

- **Report:** campaign analytics, engagement metrics, creative performance, audience insights, anomaly explanations, budget suggestions and supply forecasts.
- **Read structure:** list and search campaigns and campaign groups, creatives with previews, conversions, a campaign's structure and targeting, and media library assets.
- **Plan:** targeting facets and entities, similar entities, audience size estimates, budget pricing, targeting research for a business (from what you pass in; ADSMCP does not fetch the website), objectives, campaign types and ad-creative constraints.
- **Validate:** campaign drafts and creative assets.
- **Change (approval):**
  - create campaign groups and campaigns, including one-step image, video, text and carousel campaigns. Campaigns are created `PAUSED` and creatives `DRAFT`. If the creative step of a one-step create fails, the new campaign (and any group it created) is archived, and the result lists what was created and rolled back;
  - add campaigns to groups; add image, video, text and carousel creatives;
  - clone campaigns; reactivate archived or completed campaigns (as `PAUSED`);
  - pause, enable, archive and delete campaigns; change budgets, names, targeting, audience expansion and schedules;
  - update and delete campaign groups; pause and resume creatives; edit a creative's introductory text, call to action and landing page (re-checked when it runs: if the creative changed since staging, the edit is refused);
  - attach an existing, published lead form (`lead_form_id`) to image, video and carousel campaigns and creatives;
  - batch create groups, batch update and batch delete campaigns;
  - create conversions and associate them with campaigns.

How some inputs work:

- **`account_id`** is required on every call.
- **`status`:** create and update inputs accept only `PAUSED`. Enabling goes through `enable_linkedin_campaign`, which checks the spend cap.
- **Bidding:** automated bidding only; no bid amount is sent.
- **Media:** pass an `https` image or video URL. ADSMCP fetches it only after the change is approved, from public addresses only. Videos are streamed to LinkedIn in parts, up to 500 MB.
- **Ad copy** that contains the word "LinkedIn" is refused, following LinkedIn's ad policy.
- **`political_intent`:** LinkedIn requires the advertiser to declare it.

Not available yet: editing a creative's headline (LinkedIn does not allow it), creating lead forms, and videos over 500 MB.

## Changes need your approval

Every change below is staged as a pending action. Nothing changes in the account until a person approves it, on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page or in the confirm dialog of an [allowed AI client](/approvals#approving-in-your-ai-client); the AI model can view or reject it, never approve it. When an approved change runs, new objects are created paused or as drafts, budget changes and enables are checked against your [spend cap](/safety#spend-cap), and a failure is not retried. `remove_*`, `delete_*` and `archive_*` tools (and their `batch_` forms) also need `confirm_delete: true`.
