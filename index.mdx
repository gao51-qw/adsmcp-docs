ADSMCP is an MCP (Model Context Protocol — an open standard for connecting AI assistants to external tools) server that connects AI assistants to advertising platforms. Ask ChatGPT, Claude, or any MCP-compatible client to manage your Google Ads, Meta Ads, Amazon Ads, ChatGPT Ads, LinkedIn Ads, and TikTok Ads campaigns — no dashboards, no learning curve.
