Create a shareable live dashboard: a no-login link (shown once) that shows the chosen blocks with live data from 1 to 5 of your connected, active Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn Ads, Microsoft Ads or ChatGPT Ads accounts (not Amazon Ads: its reports are asynchronous), refreshed at most every 15 minutes, read-only. Blocks: kpi_tiles, trend_chart, campaign_table (default: all three). Date range: a preset (last_7_days, last_14_days, last_30_days, last_90_days, this_month, last_month; default last_30_days, complete days, recomputed on each refresh) or start_date + end_date (at most 90 days). Optional white-label branding (brand_name, primary_color #rrggbb, footer_text, use_workspace_logo, hide_adsmcp_branding) on plans that include it. Limits follow your effective plan (a team member uses the team owner's plan, and a team's active links count together). A metric a platform does not report is shown as unavailable, never as 0. The link expires after expires_in_days (default 30, up to your plan's limit) and can be revoked with revoke_shared_dashboard. Changes only ADSMCP's own data (no ad platform write); needs a full-permission key.

**Platform:** ADSMCP (registered under `meta`) · **Type:** Control
