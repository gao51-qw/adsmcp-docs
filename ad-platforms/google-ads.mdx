ADSMCP connects your AI client to Google Ads: accounts, campaigns, ad groups, ads, keywords, assets, audiences and conversions. Your own AI model does the reasoning; ADSMCP returns the data and stages the changes.

## Connect

Connect Google Ads on [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections) (OAuth, `adwords` scope; Google may ask for a [passkey](/knowledge-base/google-passkey)). Then call `get_connections_status`, `get_google_api_readiness` and `get_google_ads_accounts`.

- Pass the customer id as `account_id` (digits, as a string). Add `login_customer_id` when you reach a client account through a manager account.
- Campaign creates take `budget_micros` (1,000,000 micros = 1 unit of the account currency). `update_google_campaign_budget` takes `budget_amount` in normal units.

## What you can do

- **Report:** `get_google_ads_performance_report`, ad group / ad / keyword performance, budgets and bid strategies.
- **Analyze:** `analyze_search_terms` and `analyze_wasted_spend` score the rows you pass in; `explain_google_performance_anomaly` and `optimize_google_budget_allocation` work from account data.
- **Plan:** keyword ideas (`get_google_keyword_ideas`: keyword, monthly searches, competition), search theme ideas, locations, languages and audiences.
- **Validate:** Search, Display, Performance Max and YouTube / Demand Gen drafts and assets.
- **Read structure and diagnostics:** campaign structure, ad creatives, policy violations, placement performance, reach and frequency, conversion action performance, campaign dates, tracking templates, labels, frequency caps, and ad group demographics, audiences and device bids. Also Performance Max asset groups, assets, asset performance labels (Best / Good / Low), conversion goals, search themes, audience signals, and the PMax search terms and search categories reports. Also Shopping settings, Merchant Center links, the Shopping product partition tree and PMax listing filters (read only).
- **Change (approval):**
  - create Search, Performance Max, Display, YouTube, Demand Gen, Shopping and App campaigns (paused). `create_search_campaign` builds the whole structure (ad groups with keywords and responsive search ads, locations, languages and negatives) in one paused, all-or-nothing change;
  - ad groups, responsive search / display / video ads, keywords and negatives, negative keyword lists, sitelinks / callouts / calls / snippets, audiences and conversion actions;
  - Display ad groups, topics, placements and content keywords; Demand Gen ad groups, ads, audiences and channel controls; Shopping ad groups and product ads; App ad assets;
  - PMax asset groups, assets, images, search themes and audience signals (one signal per asset group);
  - labels, frequency caps, call assets, proximity and location targeting, ad schedules, placement exclusions, and business name and image assets;
  - pause, enable, budget, bidding and keyword bid changes;
  - remove keywords, ads, ad groups, campaigns and targeting criteria;
  - Shopping product groups (`add_product_group`, `update_product_group`, `remove_product_group`) and PMax listing filters (`add_asset_group_listing_filter`, `remove_asset_group_listing_filter`). Each change is sent as one request that leaves the tree valid; removing the root or an "everything else" node is refused;
  - `link_merchant_center`: links a Merchant Center account from the Google Ads side. It accepts a pending invitation from that Merchant Center account, or requests a direct link (Google allows that only if you administer both accounts);
  - `unlink_merchant_center`: removes a Merchant Center link. Shopping and Performance Max campaigns stop serving those products, so it needs `confirm_delete: true` like a delete; the link is re-read when the change runs, and nothing is sent if it changed or is gone;
  - `create_youtube_campaign`: a paused Demand Gen campaign on YouTube (in-feed, in-stream and Shorts) with its video ad, in one change.
- **Help without an account:** `select_google_campaign_type` and `help_user_upload` return structured guidance and call no Google API.

How some inputs work:

- **`status`:** update inputs accept only `PAUSED`. Turning things back on goes through the `enable_google_*` tools, which check the spend cap. Call assets are the exception: they can be set to `ENABLED`, because they carry no spend.
- **Images** are referenced by an existing asset id. Image URLs are not accepted.
- **Cost per conversion action:** Google does not split cost by conversion action, so this is the scope's total cost divided by that action's conversions.

Not available yet: Google Ads features not listed below. See **Not supported yet** in the [API reference](/api-reference/introduction).

## Changes need your approval

Every change below is staged as a pending action. Nothing changes in the account until a person approves it, on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page or in the confirm dialog of an [allowed AI client](/approvals#approving-in-your-ai-client); the AI model can view or reject it, never approve it. When an approved change runs, new objects are created paused or as drafts, budget changes and enables are checked against your [spend cap](/safety#spend-cap), and a failure is not retried. `remove_*`, `delete_*` and `archive_*` tools (and their `batch_` forms) also need `confirm_delete: true`.
