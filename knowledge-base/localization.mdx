---
title: "Languages"
icon: "globe"
description: "Which parts of ADSMCP are in English, and how to work with it in your own language."
---

- **The dashboard and these docs** are in English.
- **Your AI client** answers in whatever language you write in. ADSMCP returns data as JSON; your own model turns it into prose in your language.
- **`search_tools`** on the MCP server accepts queries in English or Chinese.
- **Ad copy** in another language: write the brand voice and banned terms in `CLAUDE.md` in that language and ask for copy in it. ADSMCP's `generate_ad_copy_ideas` returns your stored constraints; your model writes the copy.
- **Platform data** (campaign names, targeting names) comes back as the ad platform stores it.
