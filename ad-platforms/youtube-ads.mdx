YouTube ads run through Google Ads, on the same connection. See [Google Ads](/ad-platforms/google-ads).

## Order

1. `get_connections_status` and `get_google_ads_accounts`.
2. [`validate_youtube_video_asset`](/api-reference/google-ads/validate-youtube-video-asset) checks the video metadata you pass in (it does not download or inspect the video).
3. [`validate_google_youtube_demand_gen_draft`](/api-reference/google-ads/validate-google-youtube-demand-gen-draft) checks the campaign draft.
4. [`create_google_youtube_campaign`](/api-reference/google-ads/create-google-youtube-campaign) stages a paused video campaign.
5. [`create_google_ad_group`](/api-reference/google-ads/create-google-ad-group) and [`create_google_video_ad`](/api-reference/google-ads/create-google-video-ad) stage the ad group and the video ad. The video must already be on YouTube.
6. [`enable_google_campaign`](/api-reference/google-ads/enable-google-campaign) is a separate change.

For Demand Gen campaigns that also run on YouTube, see [Demand Gen](/ad-platforms/google-demand-gen).

## Changes need your approval

Every create and enable is staged as a pending action and runs only after a person approves it on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page. Campaigns and ads are created paused; enabling is checked against your [spend cap](/safety#spend-cap).

Not available yet: uploading videos to YouTube, and YouTube-specific bidding or targeting controls beyond what the tools above accept.
