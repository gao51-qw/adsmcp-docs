Your subscription and today's usage are on [Usage & Billing](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/billing). `get_usage_status` returns the same numbers from any AI client and is not counted as a call.

## One plan

| | Monthly | Yearly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ADSMCP Pro** | $9.90 a month | $100 a year (save $19) |

Every tool on every platform is included. Tool calls are not billed one by one and there are no tiers, overage charges or call pools. The yearly price is the same plan billed once a year.

Without a subscription you can sign in, connect ad accounts and create API keys, but tool calls are refused with `subscription_required` until you start the trial or subscribe.

## Free trial

- A first subscription starts with a **7-day free trial**, monthly or yearly. Someone who signed up through a [referral link](/knowledge-base/referrals) gets **14 days**.
- The trial needs a card. Nothing is charged during the trial; when it ends, the card is charged automatically for the plan you chose ($9.90 or $100).
- Cancel before the trial ends and you are never charged.
- One trial per account. An account that had a subscription before (a trial included) pays from the first day.
- During the trial the daily limit is 100 calls (below).

## Fair-use limits

The limits only stop runaway agents and abuse. Reaching one never costs anything extra.

| | Per UTC day | Per UTC month |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trial | 100 calls | 10,000 calls |
| Paid | 1,000 calls | 10,000 calls |

- The daily count restarts at 00:00 UTC, the monthly count on the 1st (UTC).
- A [team](/teams) counts as one account: every member's calls add up.
- At a limit the call is refused (see [Over a limit](#over-a-limit)) and the account owner gets one email that month.
- If your normal work needs more, write to [support@adsmcp.si](mailto:support@adsmcp.si): the limit can be raised for your account.

## What a tool call is

Every MCP `tools/call` and every `POST /api/v1/tools/{name}/execute` counts as one call toward the fair-use limits, read or change, on every platform. Staging a change is a call. These do not count:

- Approving or rejecting on the dashboard.
- `tools/list`, `search_tools`, `get_tool_schema`, a router's `list_tools`, and `get_usage_status`.
- A call refused because the ad account is inactive (`ad_account_inactive`) or the team seat is inactive.
- Views of a [shared dashboard](/shared-dashboards) link.

All AI clients and API keys on your account (and every member of a team) count together.

## Over a limit

The call is refused with `quota_exceeded` and a `detail`:

| `detail` | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `subscription_required` | No subscription (or it ended). Start the trial or subscribe on [Usage & Billing](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/billing). |
| `daily_limit_reached` | The day's fair-use limit is reached. Calls continue at 00:00 UTC. |
| `monthly_limit_reached` | The month's fair-use limit is reached. Calls continue on the 1st (UTC). |
| `team_seat_inactive` | Your team seat is inactive. See [Teams](/teams). |

Over MCP the refusal comes back as a tool error; the REST API answers `402` with a `quota` block (see [REST API](/rest-api#quota)). If the quota service cannot answer, reads continue and changes are refused with `quota_check_unavailable`.

## Switching to yearly, cancelling

- **Monthly to yearly:** on Usage & Billing choose **Pay yearly**. You pay the yearly price less the unused part of the current month, and a new year starts that day. Yearly to monthly is not offered in the dashboard.
- **Card, invoices, cancelling:** **Manage subscription** on Usage & Billing opens Paddle's customer portal. A cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you paid for; during the trial, nothing is charged.
- In a team only the owner manages the subscription.

## Referrals

People you invite get a 14-day trial instead of 7. When an invitee pays for the first time, you get a free month: $9.90 off your next renewal. See [Refer & Earn](/knowledge-base/referrals).

## Add-ons and discounts

- **[Prompt Engineering Playbook](/agent-skills/prompt-engineering-playbook):** $39 one-time per ad account (Google Ads accounts for now), 20% off when you select all your eligible accounts. One free kit with each yearly subscription. It cannot be added to a checkout that starts a trial; buy it on its own from Expert Space.
- **Discount codes:** enter a code at checkout. One discount applies per transaction; on a Playbook bundle the bundle discount applies and a code is refused. Discounts you used are listed under **Discounts applied** on the billing page.

## Subscriptions from earlier pricing

Subscriptions bought under the earlier Plus, Pro or Max plans keep their price until they end, and get the same features and fair-use limits as ADSMCP Pro.

## AI model costs

ADSMCP does not include or resell an AI model. Your AI client (Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, …) runs on your own subscription or API key. Dashboard features that write text use the provider key you add in [Settings](/settings).

## Payments and refunds

Payments are processed by Paddle. Cancel during the trial and you are never charged. Within 14 days of your first charge you can request a full refund; see the [refund policy](https://adsmcp.si/refund). A Playbook kit is not refundable once it has been built; a failed build can be retried free or refunded through support.

Billing questions: [sales@adsmcp.si](mailto:sales@adsmcp.si).

## Self-hosting

Running ADSMCP on your own infrastructure keeps the database, ad-account grants and audit log there. The approval, spend-cap and delete rules still apply.
