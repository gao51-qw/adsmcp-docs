title: "Google Ads keyword research from ChatGPT"
description: "Use ChatGPT (or any AI client) with ADSMCP to pull Google Ads keyword ideas and plan a Search campaign."
---

ADSMCP gives your AI client Google Ads keyword data; your client's model does the grouping and reasoning.

## Set up

1. Add ADSMCP to ChatGPT as a custom connector ([ChatGPT](/ai-clients/chatgpt)), or use any other [AI client](/ai-clients).
2. Connect Google Ads on [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections).

## Pull keyword ideas

Ask for ideas with seed keywords or your landing page. The agent calls [`get_google_keyword_ideas`](/api-reference/google-ads/get-google-keyword-ideas) with `account_id`, `seed_keywords` and/or `final_url`, and optional `location_ids` and `language_id` (find them with `resolve_google_locations` and `list_google_languages`).

Each idea comes back with the keyword text, average monthly searches and competition level. It does not include CPC bid estimates or an intent label: ask your AI model to group the ideas by intent, and judge cost from your own data.

## From ideas to a campaign

1. Ask the model to pick keywords and write ad copy that follows your `CLAUDE.md`.
2. `validate_google_search_campaign_draft` checks the draft.
3. `create_search_campaign` stages a paused Search campaign; `add_google_keywords` and `create_google_rsa_ad` stage the keywords and ads.

Every change is staged as a pending action; nothing happens in the ad account until a person approves it on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page.
