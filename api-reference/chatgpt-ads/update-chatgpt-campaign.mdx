<Warning>
**Change — approval required.** Calling this tool does not change your ad account. ADSMCP checks the arguments and stages the change as a pending action. It runs only after a person approves it, on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page or in the confirm dialog of an [allowed AI client](/approvals#approving-in-your-ai-client). The AI model itself can view (`list_pending_actions`) or reject (`manage_action`) it, never approve it. `execution_mode` and similar arguments do not skip the queue.

**Spend cap.** When the approved change runs, ADSMCP adds up the daily budgets of everything that would be spending in the platform's connected accounts after the change. If that total is above your Max Daily Budget (dashboard [Brand](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/brand) page), the change is refused. If the total cannot be read, it is refused too.

A change that fails when it runs is not retried automatically. It shows on the dashboard Execution Queue.
</Warning>

Change a ChatGPT Ads campaign's budget (daily / lifetime, account currency, major units), schedule (start_time, end_time or clear_end_time), targeting or conversion goals; give at least one. Targeting changes only the side you name: countries + location_ids (ids from chatgpt_geo_lookup) replace the included locations, exclude_countries + exclude_location_ids the excluded ones, and audiences / surfaces likewise; clear a side with clear_targeting, never with an empty list. The bidding type cannot be changed after creation. ADSMCP refuses the change if an edited field changed after you asked, and checks OpenAI applied it. Submitted for approval; a person approves it in the ADSMCP dashboard before it runs. Checked against the spend cap.

**Platform:** ChatGPT Ads · **Type:** Change
