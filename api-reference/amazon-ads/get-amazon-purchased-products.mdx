Cross-sell view: products shoppers bought (other than the advertised SKU) after clicking a Sponsored Products ad, per advertised and purchased ASIN, paged. Reads a live Amazon Reporting v3 report (14-day attribution): if Amazon has not finished it the result is status 'pending' with a report_id and no rows; call again with that report_id. Sponsored Products only; the date window is at most 93 days (read as up to 3 reports of at most 31 days).

**Platform:** Amazon Ads · **Type:** Read
