## What is ADSMCP?

An MCP server and dashboard that gives your AI client ad-account data, analysis tools and safe change proposals for Google, Meta, Amazon, TikTok, LinkedIn, ChatGPT Ads and Microsoft Ads. The AI model is always your own; ADSMCP does not provide one.

## Where is the MCP server?

`https://mcp.adsmcp.si/mcp`. The dashboard is [https://adsmcp.si](https://adsmcp.si). Docs search for coding agents is `https://docs.adsmcp.si/mcp`, which cannot call ad tools.

## Why did the client connect but the account is empty?

Signing in the AI client and connecting an ad account are different steps. Open [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections), connect the platform, then call `get_connections_status`.

## Can my AI agent change my campaigns?

It can propose changes. Every change (create, pause, enable, budget, bid, delete) is staged as a pending action. Only a person approves it, on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page. The agent can list and reject pending actions but cannot approve them, in chat or over the API.

## Why is the new campaign not spending?

New campaigns are created paused. Turning one on is a separate change that also needs your approval on the dashboard, and is checked against your [spend cap](/safety#spend-cap).

## A change failed. Will it retry?

No. A change that fails when it runs is not retried automatically. It shows on the dashboard [Execution Queue](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/execution-queue). Fix the cause and stage a corrected change. See [Approvals](/approvals).

## How do I delete something?

`remove_*`, `delete_*` and `archive_*` tools (and their `batch_` forms) need `confirm_delete: true`. The delete is then staged and still needs approval on the dashboard. Once approved it cannot be undone.

## What happens at the quota limit?

The call is refused with `quota_exceeded` (the REST API answers `402`). On Plus monthly, overage continues at $0.50 a call up to $50 per billing period unless you switched it off. On Plus and Pro yearly, the plan is billed again 48 hours after the "limit reached" email unless overage is off, and a larger pool starts. `get_usage_status` shows your usage. Changes are also blocked when the quota check cannot be reached. See [Pricing](/pricing).

## Can I stop overage charges?

Yes. Switch overage off on [Usage & Billing](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/billing). Nothing extra is charged and tool calls stop at your plan's limit. Switching it back on never charges you by itself.

## Why does a tool say my account is inactive?

Your plan allows a limited number of active ad accounts per platform (1 on Free and on Plus or Pro monthly, 5 on Plus or Pro yearly and on Max). Choose which ones are active on [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections). See [Multiple accounts](/accounts#active-accounts).

## Can my team share one plan?

Yes, on Plus, Pro and Max: invite people from [Team](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/team). Everyone keeps their own login and ad-account connections, and their calls come out of the owner's pool. Seats: Plus 1, Pro 3, Max 10, owner included. For more, [book a demo](https://adsmcp.si/book-demo). See [Teams](/teams).

## How do I get bonus calls?

Invite people from [Refer & Earn](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/referrals): they get 25 calls at sign-up and you get 50 after their first tool call. See [Refer & Earn](/knowledge-base/referrals).

## Can I share a dashboard with a client?

Yes. A [shared dashboard](/shared-dashboards) is a no-login, read-only link with live numbers from up to five Google Ads, Meta or TikTok accounts. It expires, and you can revoke it at any time.

## Is the dashboard available in my language?

The website and dashboard are in 13 languages, including Arabic and Hebrew with a right-to-left layout. See [Languages](/knowledge-base/localization).

## Which clients have slash commands?

Claude Code (via the `adsmcp` plugin or the installer) and Gemini CLI (via the ADSMCP extension) get `/adsmcp:setup`, `/adsmcp:performance-review`, `/adsmcp:write-ad-copy`, `/adsmcp:wasted-spend` and `/adsmcp:campaign`. Codex, Cursor and OpenClaw get the same workflows as [skills](/agent-skills). Other clients use the same steps in plain language. See [Workflows](/workflows).

## Which account will the agent use?

If more than one account is connected, name the account id. `list_connected_accounts` lists them. See [Multiple accounts](/accounts).

## Can I use ChatGPT Ads?

Yes. You can read performance, structure, audiences and pixels. With approval you can create campaigns, ad groups and ads (paused) or launch an ad in one step, upload creatives, set up audiences, pixels and conversions, and pause, resume, archive or change budgets and bids. See [ChatGPT Ads](/ad-platforms/chatgpt-ads). ChatGPT can also be the AI client that calls ADSMCP.

## Does ADSMCP use its own AI model?

No. Your AI client (Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor, …) does the reasoning and writing with your own model and account. ADSMCP returns data and computed analysis.

## The call failed. Where do I look?

Use [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting). Match `invalid_token`, `quota_exceeded`, `approval_requires_dashboard`, `spend_cap_exceeded` or the platform's error before you try again.
