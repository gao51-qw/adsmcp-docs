## Example prompts

```text
Audit my Meta campaigns from last week. Which ones have a CPA 20% higher than average?
```

```text
Scale the budget of the 'Summer Sale' campaign by 15% if its ROAS is above 3.5.
```

## Connection flow

1. **Authenticate.** Open the [Connections page](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections) and click Connect Meta.
2. **Grant permissions.** Accept the Facebook OAuth prompt for `ads_read` and `ads_management`.
3. **Verify.** Run `mcp-invoke meta get_ad_accounts` to confirm access.
