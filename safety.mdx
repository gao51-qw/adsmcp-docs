These rules apply to every client, every workflow and the REST API. A prompt cannot turn them off.

## Before any platform call

Call `get_connections_status` first. If the platform is not connected, stop. Do not guess an account id. Pass account, campaign, ad set, ad group, ad, creative, catalog and pixel ids as strings.

## Every change needs a person

- Every change to an ad account is staged as a pending action. It runs only after a person approves it on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page.
- MCP and REST can view or reject a pending action. Approving, executing or retrying through them is refused with `approval_requires_dashboard`. There is no chat approval.
- `execution_mode` and similar arguments do not change this.

See [Approvals](/approvals).

## Paused creates

New campaigns, ad groups, ads and creatives are created paused or as drafts (LinkedIn creatives as `DRAFT`, Klaviyo campaigns as drafts). Turning anything on is a separate change that needs its own approval.

## Spend cap

Set a **Max Daily Budget** on the dashboard [Brand](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/brand) page. It applies to each platform's account total separately.

When an approved budget change or enable runs, ADSMCP:

1. Reads the daily budgets of everything that is spending across the platform's connected accounts.
2. Applies the change to that snapshot.
3. Refuses the change (`account_cap_exceeded` / `spend_cap_exceeded`) if the total would be over the cap.

If any account, budget or currency cannot be read, the change is refused (`account_budget_unreadable`, `spend_cap_unreadable`) rather than allowed. Lifetime budgets count as their daily share. A cap of 0 means no cap is set.

## Deletes need confirmation

`remove_*` and `delete_*` tools are refused unless the call passes `confirm_delete: true`. With it, the delete is staged and still needs approval. Once approved it cannot be undone.

## Failures are not retried

A change that fails when it runs stops. Nothing retries automatically. Read the error, fix the cause, and stage a corrected change.

## Missing targets

If `STRATEGY.md` is missing a KPI the workflow needs, the agent stops the comparison. It does not substitute an example CPA, ROAS, CTR or daily budget. Run [/adsmcp:setup](/workflows) and fill the target first.

## What gets remembered

`record_learning` stores a confirmed setup choice, an approved action result, a read result from the current run, or a traced failure. A projection or an unapproved recommendation is not recorded as performance or savings.

## Platform APIs stay on the server

Your AI client never calls the ad platforms directly (`graph.facebook.com`, `googleads.googleapis.com`, `business-api.tiktok.com`, LinkedIn, Amazon Ads or ChatGPT Ads hosts). Those calls happen inside the ADSMCP service, with your connected accounts.

## Your model

ADSMCP supplies data and analysis. Decisions and wording come from your own AI model, and every change still ends at a person on the dashboard.
