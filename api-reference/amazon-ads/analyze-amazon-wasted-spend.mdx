Find Sponsored Products targets (default) or search terms that spend without converting, and ones whose ACOS is at or above a threshold. Search-term results carry a negative_candidate object per row for the caller to confirm and stage; nothing is applied. Reads a live Amazon Reporting v3 report (14-day attribution): if Amazon has not finished it the result is status 'pending' with a report_id and no rows; call again with that report_id. Sponsored Products only; the date window is at most 93 days (read as up to 3 reports of at most 31 days).

**Platform:** Amazon Ads · **Type:** Read
