---
title: "Google asks for a passkey when connecting Google Ads"
icon: "key"
description: "Why Google's sign-in may ask for a passkey while you connect Google Ads to ADSMCP, and what to do."
---

When you connect Google Ads on [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections), Google's own sign-in can ask you to confirm with a **passkey** (or another strong sign-in method) before it sends you back to ADSMCP. That prompt comes from Google, not from ADSMCP.

## What to do

1. Finish Google's prompt with the passkey on your device or security key.
2. Approve the Google Ads access ADSMCP asks for (`adwords` scope).
3. Back on Connections, confirm the Google Ads account is listed, then ask your AI client to call `get_connections_status`.

Google's own guide: [Use a passkey with Google Ads](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16968810).

## If it does not work

- **You have no passkey yet:** create one in your Google Account security settings, then connect again.
- **The passkey worked but ADSMCP did not connect:** try again in a private browser window, and make sure you sign in with the Google account that has access to the Google Ads account.
- **Someone else manages the Google Ads account:** ask them to connect it, or to give your Google account access first.

## What ADSMCP cannot do

ADSMCP cannot skip, create or store your passkey. It never sees it: the passkey stays between you and Google. An existing ADSMCP connection keeps working; the prompt appears when Google asks you to sign in again.

See [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting) or contact [support](/support).
