ChatGPT Ads are ads that run inside ChatGPT. ADSMCP reads them and stages changes to existing campaigns, ad groups and ads. (Using ChatGPT as your AI client is separate; see [ChatGPT](/ai-clients/chatgpt).)

## Connect

On [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections), paste the ChatGPT Ads API key for each ad account you want to connect. Then call `get_connections_status` and `list_chatgpt_accounts`. Pass the account id (`adacct_...`) on later calls.

## What you can do

- **Report:** account performance, insights by level (with country or device segments, filters and sorting) and conversion insights.
- **Read structure:** campaigns, ad groups and ads.
- **Change (approval):** pause and resume campaigns, ad groups and ads; change a campaign budget (normal currency units, at least 1); change an ad group's max bid.

Not available yet: creating, archiving or launching campaigns, ad groups or ads; creative uploads; custom audiences; pixels, conversion events and Conversions API settings.

## Changes need your approval

Every change below is staged as a pending action. Nothing changes in the account until a person approves it on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page; your AI client can view or reject it, never approve it. When an approved change runs, new objects are created paused or as drafts, budget changes and enables are checked against your [spend cap](/safety#spend-cap), and a failure is not retried. `remove_*` / `delete_*` tools also need `confirm_delete: true`.
