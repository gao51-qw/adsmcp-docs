> **A free skill for your agent**
>
> Agent instructions and eight ready-to-use prompts in one file. Keep it, customize it, or share this page with a friend.

## Add to your agent

Download the file, choose your agent below, then try a starter prompt. The prompts are also at the top of the skill document, ready to copy and paste.

**Claude**

**1. Open Skills**

Open **Settings → Customize → Skills**, then choose **Upload skill**.

**2. Upload your download**

Choose **SKILL.md** and click **Save**. You can upload the Markdown file directly.

![Claude Upload skill screen showing support for Markdown files with a YAML skill name and description](/images/M0GNQwpJ_SUIcx_O/images/free-skill/claude-upload-skill.png)

Upload the Markdown file in Claude's Skills settings.

**3. Try a prompt**

Open a conversation and paste one of the [starter prompts below](#try-a-starter-prompt).

**ChatGPT**

**1. Open Skills**

Open **Plugins → Skills**, then click **+**.

**2. Upload your download**

Choose **Upload from your computer** and select **SKILL.md**. Complete the save flow shown in your workspace.

If you downloaded an earlier copy, rename it to exactly **SKILL.md** before uploading.

![ChatGPT Plugins and Skills page with Upload from your computer selected in the plus menu](/images/M0GNQwpJ_SUIcx_O/images/free-skill/chatgpt-skills.png)

Use the + menu on ChatGPT's Skills tab to upload your skill.

**3. Try a prompt**

Open a conversation and paste one of the [starter prompts below](#try-a-starter-prompt).

**Coding agent**

**1. Open your repository**

Open the local repository where you want to use the skill in Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, or your preferred coding agent.

**2. Attach the file and ask your agent to install it**

Attach **SKILL.md**, then copy this message:

Install the free skill in your local repository

Read the attached paid media skill and add it to this local repository using your supported skills format. Keep the included starter prompts. Preserve existing repository instructions, avoid duplicate installations, and tell me where you saved the skill.

**3. Try a prompt**

Once your agent confirms where it saved the skill, paste one of the [starter prompts below](#try-a-starter-prompt).

## Try a starter prompt

These are the same eight prompts included in your download. Pick one and paste it into your agent after adding the skill. They ask for analysis or drafts; they do not ask your agent to change live ads.

> **Understand performance**
>
> Starter prompt 1
>
> Using ADSMCP, review the last 30 complete days for one connected ad account. Show spend, conversions, and cost per conversion, then give me the three most useful things to investigate. Ask which account if needed.
>
> Starter prompt 2
>
> Using ADSMCP, compare the last 7 complete days with the previous 7 for one connected ad account. What changed most, and what evidence could explain it? Separate observed changes from possible causes.

> **Reduce wasted spend**
>
> Starter prompt 3
>
> Using ADSMCP, find the strongest signs of wasted spend in one connected ad account over the last 30 complete days. Rank up to three opportunities by evidence and spend affected. Recommend changes for review; do not apply them.
>
> Starter prompt 4
>
> Using ADSMCP, review how budget is split across campaigns in one connected ad account. Ask for my goal and target if needed, then propose a reallocation within the current total budget. Show tradeoffs; do not change anything.

> **Plan a campaign**
>
> Starter prompt 5
>
> Help me plan a campaign using ADSMCP. Ask for my offer, audience, location, goal, and budget, then recommend a suitable connected platform and a campaign structure. Keep this as a draft for review.
>
> Starter prompt 6
>
> Help me review a campaign idea before launch. Check the offer, landing page, audience, creative, budget, and measurement plan. Use ADSMCP data where available, identify what is missing, and give me a launch checklist. Do not create or activate anything.

> **Improve creative**
>
> Starter prompt 7
>
> Help me write three ad-copy directions for my offer and chosen platform. Use ADSMCP to review existing ads if available; ask for the offer and brand voice if needed. Explain what each direction tests and keep all copy as drafts.
>
> Starter prompt 8
>
> Using ADSMCP, check whether creative fatigue could explain weaker performance in one connected ad account. Use the metrics available on that platform, explain the limits of the evidence, and suggest a creative test. Do not change any ads.
