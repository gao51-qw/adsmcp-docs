Material for writing RSA copy for one campaign: Google's limits and editorial rules, the three headline categories with your inputs slotted in, keyword themes from the campaign's enabled keywords, existing headlines and descriptions with Google's performance labels and ad strength, and the final URLs. Returns no finished copy: suggested_headlines and suggested_descriptions are empty, and your own model writes them. Read-only; fetches no URL.

**Platform:** Google Ads · **Type:** Read

**Also accepted as:** `suggest_ad_content`. These older names run this same tool.
