<Warning>
**Change — approval required.** Calling this tool does not change your ad account. ADSMCP checks the arguments and stages the change as a pending action. It runs only after a person approves it on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page. Over MCP or REST you can view (`list_pending_actions`) or reject (`manage_action`) it, never approve it. `execution_mode` and similar arguments do not skip the queue.

**Spend cap.** When the approved change runs, ADSMCP adds up the daily budgets of everything that would be spending in the platform's connected accounts after the change. If that total is above your Max Daily Budget (dashboard [Brand](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/brand) page), the change is refused. If the total cannot be read, it is refused too.

A change that fails when it runs is not retried automatically. It shows on the dashboard Execution Queue.
</Warning>

Change an existing Meta campaign, written for Advantage+ catalog campaigns: name, status, daily or lifetime budget (minor units), spend cap, bid strategy, schedule, or the product catalog (sent as promoted_object.product_catalog_id). Staged for dashboard approval; nothing changes until it is approved. Budget changes, a shorter schedule on a lifetime budget, and ACTIVE are checked against the account-total spend cap.
