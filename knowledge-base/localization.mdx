## How the language is chosen

1. The language you picked last, on this browser.
2. Otherwise your browser's language: an exact match, then a close one (`zh-HK` and `zh-Hant` show Traditional Chinese, `pt-PT` shows Portuguese (Brasil), `es-MX` shows Spanish).
3. Otherwise English.

## Changing it

- On the website, use the language switcher in the header.
- In the dashboard, open [Settings → Preferences](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/settings?tab=preferences).

The page switches at once. When you are signed in, the choice is saved to your account, so the dashboard opens in the same language on your other devices.

Arabic and Hebrew use a right-to-left layout. Numbers in Arabic use Latin digits so they match the ad platforms' own screens. Dates, numbers and currencies follow the language's conventions; the currency itself is always your ad account's currency.

## What stays in English

- Parts of the dashboard not translated yet show English text.
- The terms, privacy, refund and cookie policies are in English, and the English text governs; other languages show a short notice saying so.
- These docs, tool names, tool results and API error messages are in English.
- Page addresses do not change with the language, and search engines see the English pages.

Changing the interface language never changes your ad targeting.

## Working with your AI client in your language

- Your AI client answers in the language you write in. ADSMCP returns data as JSON; your own model turns it into prose.
- `search_tools` on the MCP server accepts queries in English or Chinese.
- For ad copy in another language, write the brand voice and banned terms in `CLAUDE.md` in that language and ask for copy in it. `generate_ad_copy_ideas` returns your stored constraints; your model writes the copy.
- Platform data (campaign names, targeting names) comes back as the ad platform stores it.
