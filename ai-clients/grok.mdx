## Setup

**1. Create a bot for paid media**

Sign in to Grok Bot with your Cursor account. You also need an ADSMCP account — [free to start](https://adsmcp.si), no card.

Choose **Create your own** and give the bot a short name, one title, and a description of the work it does. Keep one bot per job — a bot that does ads and nothing else gives better answers than a general one.

Paste this as the description and edit the specifics:

```
Title: Paid media manager

Manage paid media across Google Ads, Meta Ads, Amazon Ads, LinkedIn Ads,
TikTok Ads, and ChatGPT Ads through the ADSMCP plugin. Always pull live
data before answering a performance question — never answer from memory.

Before creating or changing anything: check which accounts are connected,
research first, and show me the data you based the recommendation on.
Create every campaign paused. Ask me before anything that changes spend —
new campaigns, budget changes, bid changes. Never delete a campaign.

Our brand: [brand name, what you sell, who you sell to]
Voice: [tone, words to avoid]
Guardrails: [monthly budget ceiling, target CPA or ROAS]
```

**2. Open the Marketplace**

Select **Marketplace** at the bottom of the sidebar, or follow a **Connect** card in chat. On the mobile app, tap your avatar in the top left.

![Grok Bot window with the Marketplace button at the bottom of the left sidebar](/images/xQY-jlSRqCTgZmaL/images/grok/marketplace-sidebar.png)

Marketplace sits at the bottom of the Grok Bot sidebar, under your bots

**3. Add ADSMCP**

Search for **adsmcp** and add it. Or open the [ADSMCP for Grok Bot](https://x.ai/bot/plugin/66672239) page and click **Add to Grok Bot**. If Grok Bot isn't installed on this computer, the page offers **Download for macOS** first.

It's the same [marketplace listing](https://cursor.com/marketplace/adsmcp) that Cursor installs from, **Verified by Cursor**.

**4. Authorize**

When Grok Bot asks you to **Authorize**, finish the ADSMCP sign-in in your browser. If it shows **Waiting for authorization**, use **Reopen** to bring the browser tab back.

Confirm ADSMCP appears under **Installed**.

**5. Connect your ad accounts**

Connect the platforms you want the bot to manage at [adsmcp.com/connections](https://adsmcp.si/connections). The bot can only reach accounts you've connected there.

**6. Ask it something read-only first**

Safe first task — confirms the connection without changing anything.

Which ad accounts am I connected to, and what did each one spend in the last 30 days?

> [!TIP]
>
> If the bot answers with your real account names and real spend figures, you're connected.

## Example Prompts

### Monday Morning Scorecard

Cross-platform performance review with the three things to fix.

Pull last week's performance from every connected platform. Give me one table with spend, conversions, CPA, and ROAS per platform, flag anything that moved more than 20% week over week, and end with the top 3 actions you'd take.

### Find Wasted Spend

Cross-platform waste audit with a savings estimate.

Find wasted spend across all my platforms. Show me keywords with spend and zero conversions, Meta ads with rising frequency and falling CTR, and how much I'd save per month by cutting them. Don't change anything yet — just show me the list.

### Rewrite Underperforming Ad Copy

New headlines grounded in real search terms.

Find my lowest-CTR Google Search ad group, pull the search terms that actually converted in it over the last 60 days, and write 8 new headlines and 3 descriptions based on them. Keep to Google's character limits and my brand voice.

### Audit Before You Launch

Conversion tracking check before spending on a new campaign.

Before I launch anything new, audit my conversion tracking on Google and Meta. Tell me which conversion actions are firing, which ones haven't recorded a conversion in 30 days, and what you'd fix first.

### Plan a New Channel

Budget split across channels for a defined goal.

I have \$6,000/month to spend on demand gen for a B2B SaaS product selling to IT directors. Based on what my existing campaigns already tell us about cost and intent, recommend how to split that across Google Search, LinkedIn, and Meta. Then build the LinkedIn campaign paused so I can review it.

## Scheduled Reports With Routines

A routine tells one bot when to run a workflow — and it runs in the cloud, so your laptop can be closed. This is the reason to put ad reporting in Grok Bot rather than only in your editor.

**1. Open the routine panel**

Open the bot, choose **View conversation details**, then **Routines**.

**2. Write the instruction**

Give the routine a name and the instruction you want run on a schedule:

```
Pull last week's performance from every connected ADSMCP platform.
Post a scorecard: spend, conversions, CPA, ROAS per platform, week over
week. List anything that moved more than 20%. End with the top 3 actions.
Read-only — do not change any campaign.
```

**3. Set when it runs**

Open **When to run** and pick a schedule — **Every day**, **Weekdays**, **Every week**, **Every month**, or an interval. The clock time follows the **Timezone** in the bot's settings, not your browser's.

**4. Turn Active on**

An inactive routine stays saved and never fires. Saving doesn't run it either — the first run is the next matching time.

> [!WARNING]
>
> Every run spends ADSMCP tool calls and Grok Bot usage. A weekly scorecard is cheap; an hourly one is not. Start weekly, check the run history, and widen or narrow from there. See [pricing](/knowledge-base/pricing) for your tool call allowance.

ADSMCP also has its own scheduling tools, which deliver briefs independently of any client. See [scheduled tasks](/guides/scheduled-tasks) for when to use which.

## Safety

* **Campaigns are created paused.** Nothing spends until you enable it.
* **Read each approval card.** Grok Bot shows one before a tool takes action. Use **Allow once** while you learn how the bot handles a workflow, and **Deny** when the proposed action isn't what you asked for.
* **Spend-affecting writes stop for confirmation** — campaign creation, budget changes, bid changes.
* **Campaigns are never deleted automatically.**
* **Never paste credentials into chat.** ADSMCP uses OAuth, so it never asks for one. Your ad platform credentials stay server-side with ADSMCP and are scoped to the accounts you connect.

## Grok Bot vs. Cursor

Same plugin, same marketplace listing, two different products. Installing in one does not install in the other.

| | **Grok Bot** | **[Cursor](/ai-clients/cursor)** |
| - | - | - |
| **Where it runs** | Cloud — desktop, web, mobile | Your machine, inside the editor |
| **Install from** | **Marketplace** in the sidebar | **Customize** in the sidebar |
| **Scheduling** | Routines, run in the cloud | ADSMCP's own scheduling tools |
| **Brand context** | The bot's description | `BRAND.md` and `STRATEGY.md` in your project folder |
| **Skills** | All 14 | All 14 |
| **Workflow enforcement** | What you write in the description | Two rules that load automatically |
| **Best for** | Recurring reporting and optimization you want to arrive on its own | Hands-on campaign building alongside your files |

> [!NOTE]
>
> All 14 skills install in Grok Bot. What doesn't come across is the subagent and the two `.mdc` rules — those are Cursor plugin components. In Grok Bot the bot's description does that job, which is why the setup step above is worth filling in properly rather than leaving generic.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
| - | - |
| ADSMCP isn't in the plugin list | Search `adsmcp` (lowercase, one word) in **Marketplace**. |
| "Disabled by team admin" | If you use Grok Bot through a Cursor team, your Cursor team admin controls which marketplace plugins are available. Ask them to enable ADSMCP. |
| Stuck on "Waiting for authorization" | Use **Reopen** to bring the browser tab back and finish signing in. |
| Plugin added but no ad data | Connect your platforms at [adsmcp.com/connections](https://adsmcp.si/connections) — installing the plugin doesn't connect accounts. |
| Bot answers from memory instead of live data | Add "always pull live data before answering a performance question" to the bot's description. |
| Routine saved but **Run history** says "No runs yet" | Saving doesn't create a run. Check **Active** is on and wait for the next matching time, or use **Test run** — which does real work, so give it a read-only instruction. |
| Wrong time on a scheduled report | Schedules follow the **Timezone** in the bot's settings, not your browser's. |
| Rate limit hit | See [pricing](/knowledge-base/pricing) for your plan's tool call allowance. |

## FAQ

> **Do I need to add ADSMCP as a custom connector or MCP server?**
>
> No. Add the official [ADSMCP for Grok Bot](https://x.ai/bot/plugin/66672239) plugin from the **Marketplace**. It brings the connector, the tools, and all 14 skills in one step. If you added `https://mcp.adsmcp.si/mcp` by URL before the listing existed, remove it so your bot doesn't see two copies of every tool.

> **Do I need Cursor installed to use ADSMCP in Grok Bot?**
>
> No. Grok Bot signs in with a Cursor account and installs from the Cursor Marketplace, but it runs on its own — desktop, web, or mobile. You never have to open the editor.

> **If I installed ADSMCP in Cursor, is it already in Grok Bot?**
>
> No. Install it once in each. They're separate products that share a marketplace.

> **Can one bot manage several brands?**
>
> It can reach every ad account connected to your ADSMCP account, so technically yes — but a bot holds one role and one set of context. For agency work, give each client its own bot with its own description. See [multi-client](/knowledge-base/multi-client).

> **Can the bot spend money without asking?**
>
> No. Campaigns are created paused, and spend-affecting actions stop for an approval card. The bot proposes; you approve.

> **How much does ADSMCP cost?**
>
> Pricing is based on tool calls, not ad spend, and it's the same across every AI client. Scheduled routines consume tool calls on every run. See [pricing and plans](/knowledge-base/pricing).
