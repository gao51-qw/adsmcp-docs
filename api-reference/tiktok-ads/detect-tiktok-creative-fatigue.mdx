Hook-rate fatigue signal per ad. Reads get_tiktok_creative_analysis for a recent window and the window right before it (window_days each, ending end_date), and compares each ad's hook rate (2-second video views / impressions, derived by that tool). An ad is flagged fatigue_signal when its hook rate fell by at least decline_threshold (relative, default 0.2) and both windows have at least min_impressions (default 1000); ads with too little data are listed as insufficient_data and never flagged. Ads are ranked by hook-rate change, largest decline first. Reports the data only; it does not recommend actions. Give ad_ids, adgroup_id or adgroup_ids to narrow it. Passes through truncated / note. Read-only.

**Platform:** TikTok Ads · **Type:** Read
