ADSMCP connects your AI client to Amazon Ads: profiles, Sponsored Products (SP), Sponsored Brands (SB) and Sponsored Display (SD) campaigns, ad groups, keywords, targets and reports.

## Connect

Connect Amazon Ads on [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections) (OAuth). Then call `get_connections_status` and `get_amazon_profiles`, and pass the profile id on later calls.

- Report tools take `ad_product` `sp`, `sb` or `sd`. Amazon builds reports asynchronously: `request_amazon_campaign_report` / `get_amazon_campaign_performance` can return a report id that you fetch later with `get_amazon_report`.

## What you can do

- **Report:** campaign, search-term, product and purchased-product performance; marketplace reports.
- **Analyze:** placements, targeting, wasted spend and budget suggestions.
- **Read structure:** SP/SB/SD campaigns, ad groups, keywords and negatives, targets, product ads, portfolios, brands and moderation status.
- **Change (approval):** create SP, SB and SD campaigns (paused); pause, enable and change daily budgets of campaigns.

Not available yet: creating or editing ad groups, keywords, targets and product ads.

## Changes need your approval

Every change below is staged as a pending action. Nothing changes in the account until a person approves it on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page; your AI client can view or reject it, never approve it. When an approved change runs, new objects are created paused or as drafts, budget changes and enables are checked against your [spend cap](/safety#spend-cap), and a failure is not retried. `remove_*` / `delete_*` tools also need `confirm_delete: true`.
