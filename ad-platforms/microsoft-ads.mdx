ADSMCP connects your AI client to Microsoft Advertising (Bing): accounts, campaigns, ad groups, responsive search ads, keywords, shared budgets, bid strategies, ad extensions, audiences and targeting criteria.

## Connect

Connect Microsoft Ads on [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections) with your Microsoft sign-in. ADSMCP adds one connection for each active ad account your sign-in can reach. Then call `get_connections_status` and `list_microsoft_accounts`.

- Pass `account_id` on every change. On reads, if you have several accounts and pass no `account_id`, ADSMCP returns the account list instead of guessing.
- The dashboard's Microsoft page shows your campaigns read only.

## What you can do

- **Report:** account totals and campaign, ad group, keyword and search term performance (impressions, clicks, spend, CTR, CPC, conversions, conversion value, cost per conversion, ROAS) for a preset period or dates up to 36 months back. A report Microsoft has not finished within the call's time limit comes back as `report_pending` with a `report_token`; call again with the token to collect it.
- **Read structure:** accounts, campaigns, ad groups, ads, keywords, shared budgets, bid strategies, ad extensions, audiences, asset groups, and campaign and ad group targeting criteria.
- **Prepare:** `discover_microsoft_media` lists the images in the account, and `validate_and_prepare_microsoft_assets` checks ad assets before you create anything.
- **Change (approval):**
  - create Search campaigns, ad groups, responsive search ads and keywords (created paused);
  - add and edit sitelink, callout and structured snippet extensions in the account library, and create remarketing lists (one URL rule each);
  - create and change Performance Max asset groups (created paused). Images are passed by existing media id; each group needs a landscape and a square image;
  - add, change and delete campaign and ad group targeting criteria (locations, age, gender, device, day and time, audiences);
  - copy a Google Ads Search campaign into Microsoft Ads (see [Copy a Google Ads campaign](#copy-a-google-ads-campaign-to-microsoft-ads));
  - change campaigns, shared budgets, bid strategies, ad groups, ads and keywords: names, status, budgets, bids and targets, and the final URLs and paths of responsive search ads.

How some inputs work:

- **Targeting criteria** are created active, not paused: a paused location or audience criterion would widen who sees the ads instead of narrowing it.
- **Shared budgets:** a campaign on a shared budget spends from that budget, so its own daily budget cannot be changed; change the shared budget with `update_microsoft_budgets`.
- **Ad extension edits:** Microsoft replaces the whole extension, so ADSMCP re-reads it and resends it with only your fields changed. If the extension holds a field ADSMCP cannot carry over, the edit is refused rather than risk clearing it.
- **Spend cap:** enables, budgets, bids, bid adjustments and bid strategy changes are checked against your spend cap.

Not available yet: creating campaign types other than Search, ad types other than responsive search ads, linking ad extensions to a campaign, editing price and promotion extensions, and asset group listing groups, audience signals, search themes and videos.

## Copy a Google Ads campaign to Microsoft Ads

You can copy a Google Ads **Search** campaign into a Microsoft Advertising account as one approved action.

1. **Preview.** `preview_google_to_microsoft_copy` reads the Google campaign and returns the Microsoft plan: settings and daily budget, bidding, ad groups, keywords (match types and bids), negative keywords, responsive search ads, sitelinks, callouts and location targets. It gives counts and a `not_copied` list that names every item that cannot be carried over, and why. It makes no Microsoft Ads call and changes nothing.
2. **Copy.** `copy_google_campaign_to_microsoft` with the same arguments stages **one** pending action that holds the plan. Nothing is created until a person approves it.
3. **On approval** ADSMCP creates the pieces in this order: the campaign, its location targets, ad groups, keywords, negative keywords, responsive search ads, then sitelinks and callouts. It reports each step.

Arguments: `account_id` (the Microsoft account), `google_account_id` and `google_campaign_id` are required. These are optional: `google_login_customer_id`, `daily_budget` (default: the Google campaign's daily budget), `campaign_name`, `time_zone` (default: the Microsoft account's), `location_map`, and `include` (`negatives`, `locations`, `ad_extensions`, all on by default).

### What status things get

- The new **campaign is created Paused**, so nothing serves.
- Ad groups, keywords and ads **keep their Google status**: enabled becomes Active, paused stays Paused. Removed ones are not copied.
- Enabling the campaign later is a separate approved change. It is checked against your [spend cap](/safety#spend-cap).

### Locations

Google and Microsoft number locations differently, so ADSMCP does not guess. Pass `location_map`, a list of Google location ids with the Microsoft location id for each, for the locations you want copied. Any other location is listed in `not_copied`.

Without a mapped location, no location target is copied and the new campaign targets **all locations**. The approval says so plainly. The approval also lists each mapping you gave. ADSMCP does not check the Microsoft ids you pass.

### Rules

- Only Search campaigns. Performance Max, Display, Video, Shopping, App and Demand Gen are refused with `campaign_type_not_supported`.
- Both accounts must use the same currency, or the call is refused with `currency_mismatch`.
- Both accounts must be connected, and the Google account must be active.
- The daily budget is checked against your spend cap when you approve.
- Microsoft has no broad-match negative keywords. A one-word broad negative becomes a phrase negative. Longer ones are listed in `not_copied`.
- A sitelink is copied with both description lines or with none. A sitelink with only one line is listed in `not_copied`.
- Shared negative keyword lists are copied as the new campaign's own negatives. A later change to the Google list does not carry over.
- Not copied, and listed with a reason: audiences, demographics, ad schedules, device adjustments, radius targets, Display expansion, dynamic search ad groups, ads other than responsive search ads, responsive search ads with ad customizers or outside Microsoft's limits, unsupported bid strategies (the copy uses Enhanced CPC), and asset types other than sitelinks and callouts. Pinned headlines and descriptions are copied unpinned.
- One copy creates at most 30 ad groups, 2,000 keywords, 150 responsive search ads, 1,000 campaign negatives, 1,000 ad group negatives, 20 sitelinks, 20 callouts and 100 locations. A larger campaign is refused with `campaign_too_large`.

### If a step fails

The copy stops and reports what exists and what did not run. Nothing is deleted. A repeat of an approved copy never creates a second campaign.

When Microsoft's answer does not say whether some items were created, the step is marked **unknown** and the report says: "these items may exist; check in Microsoft Advertising before anything is created again." A step in that state is not sent again until you have checked.

## Changes need your approval

Every change below is staged as a pending action. Nothing changes in the account until a person approves it, on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page or in the confirm dialog of an [allowed AI client](/approvals#approving-in-your-ai-client); the AI model can view or reject it, never approve it. When an approved change runs, new objects are created paused or as drafts, budget changes and enables are checked against your [spend cap](/safety#spend-cap), and a failure is not retried. `remove_*`, `delete_*` and `archive_*` tools (and their `batch_` forms) also need `confirm_delete: true`.
