It runs only after a person approves it, on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page or in the confirm dialog of an [allowed AI client](/approvals#approving-in-your-ai-client). **Spend cap.** When the approved change runs, ADSMCP adds up the daily budgets of everything that would be spending in the platform's connected accounts after the change. If that total is above your Max Daily Budget (dashboard [Brand](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/brand) page), the change is refused. If the total cannot be read, it is refused too.

A change that fails when it runs is not retried automatically. It shows on the dashboard Execution Queue.

Submitted for approval; a person approves it in the ADSMCP dashboard before it runs.
