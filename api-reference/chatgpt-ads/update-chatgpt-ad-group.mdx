Change a ChatGPT Ads ad group's bidding: max bid (account currency), strategy (fixed_bid, maximize_clicks, maximize_conversions) and per-audience bid multipliers; give at least one. Unsent bid settings are kept; a maximize strategy drops the manual bid and fixed_bid needs one. ADSMCP refuses the change if the bidding changed after you asked, and checks OpenAI applied it. Submitted for approval; a person approves it in the ADSMCP dashboard before it runs. Higher bids and strategy changes: Checked against the spend cap.

**Platform:** ChatGPT Ads · **Type:** Change
