Compare each ad platform's spend and its own reported conversions and conversion value with what your GA4 property attributes to that platform's paid traffic for the same dates (sessions, key events, purchase revenue by session source / medium, using a fixed mapping such as google + cpc -\> Google Ads, facebook / instagram + paid -\> Meta). Gives platform-reported and GA4-based ROAS and CPA (GA4-based only when the spend currency equals the GA4 currency), unattributed GA4 traffic once, and notes on why the numbers differ. Amazon Ads is shown with its own report numbers and is not compared with GA4. A platform or GA4 read that fails is reported with its error code; nothing is estimated. Read-only; one tool call.

**Platform:** Google Analytics 4 · **Type:** Read
