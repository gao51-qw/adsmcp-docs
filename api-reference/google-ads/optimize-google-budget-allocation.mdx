Propose daily budgets across the account's enabled campaigns: a deterministic greedy move from weak to strong campaigns (ROAS, or 1/CPA without conversion value; budget-limited campaigns weighted up), within max_change_percentage and min_daily_budget, PAUSE for zero-conversion campaigns spending over 2× their CPA target. Read-only: it changes nothing. To apply it, stage update_google_campaign_budget / pause_google_campaign per row and approve decreases and pauses before increases. Shared budgets are reported, never moved.

**Platform:** Google Ads · **Type:** Read
