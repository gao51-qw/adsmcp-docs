Ad-level performance: spend, impressions, clicks, CTR, purchases, CPA and ROAS per ad, ranked. Give ad_ids, or parent_id (a campaign or ad set) to list its ads with get_ads; at most 25 ads, each read with get_ad_insights (one sub-call per ad, each counted against Meta quota). Ranks by ROAS (purchase value / spend) when Meta reports purchase values, else by CPA (spend / purchases), else by spend, and says which in ranked_by; entries without that metric are listed last and are not called best or worst. It reports the data and the ranking only; it does not generate recommendations. Read-only.

**Platform:** Meta Ads · **Type:** Read
