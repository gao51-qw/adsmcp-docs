Invite someone to ADSMCP: their free trial lasts **14 days instead of 7**, and you get **one free month** once they pay for ADSMCP Pro for the first time. Your link and invites are on [Refer & Earn](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/referrals).

## How it works

1. **Your link.** After your own first counted tool call, Refer & Earn shows your link, `https://adsmcp.si/r/<CODE>` (a 7-character code). It never changes and can be used any number of times.
2. **They sign up.** A new account created through your link, with a verified email address (Google sign-up counts as verified), gets a 14-day trial when it starts its subscription.
3. **They pay.** When their first real charge goes through (the trial start is not a charge), you get a free month, usually within about 15 minutes, and an email: "You earned a free month of ADSMCP Pro".

## Your free month

- A free month is **$9.90 off your next renewal**, applied as a Paddle discount. On a yearly plan it is $9.90 off the yearly renewal.
- It is applied to your subscription automatically. Free months you earn while you have no subscription wait until you start one.
- Several free months are used one renewal after another. A discount code already on your subscription is used up first.

## Email invites

After 3 counted tool calls you can also invite people by email from Refer & Earn: up to 5 addresses per send, 5 invites a day and 50 in total. An invited person who signs up later with that address is matched even without clicking the link.

- The invite is a one-time email that names you, says the address was entered by you and is not on any list, and has a link to stop ADSMCP invites to that address.
- ADSMCP does not tell you whether an address already has an account or has opted out; such addresses are skipped silently and still count toward your limits.
- One invite per address from you, and at most one invite to any address every 30 days from anyone.
- ADSMCP stores only a hash and a masked form of the address (`a***@example.com`).

## Limits

| Limit | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Rewarded referrals per UTC month | 5 |
| Rewarded referrals in total | 20 |

A referral that arrives over a limit is not rewarded, now or later. The person who signed up still keeps their 14-day trial.

## Who counts

- Only **new** accounts: created within 7 days of being attributed, after they opened your link or got your invite, with a verified email. The first link or invite they used wins (a link visit is remembered for 30 days).
- Referring yourself never pays: the same account, the same email address (including Gmail dots and `+tags`), or a disposable email domain is rejected, and neither side gets anything.
- When both sides are clearly the same person or business (the same payment details, or the same network, browser and email name), the referrer's reward is not issued. The invitee keeps their 14-day trial.
- A shared network alone (colleagues in one office) is not a rejection, but the reward may wait for a manual review.
- If the invitee's first payment is refunded or charged back, your reward is withdrawn: a free month not yet applied is canceled; one already taken off a renewal stays.

## Your page

Refer & Earn shows your link and ready-to-copy text, the invites you sent, sign-ups, the free months you earned (used and waiting) and how much of each limit is left.
