Amazon campaign performance for a date range: impressions, clicks, cost, sales, ACOS and ROAS, from Amazon Reporting v3 via get_amazon_marketplace_report. The reports are asynchronous: if Amazon has not finished one, that product is returned as status 'pending' with its report_id and NO metrics, and get_amazon_report fetches it later. Metrics are the account's totals for the period per ad product (Amazon's summary), not a per campaign breakdown. Without ad_product, SP, SB and SD are each requested and returned tagged with their ad_product (this creates three reports). Cost is Amazon's cost column (spend); ACOS and ROAS are null when there is no sales or no cost.

**Platform:** Amazon Ads · **Type:** Read
