Country-level performance: an AUDIENCE report by country_code through get_tiktok_campaign_performance_report (spend, impressions, clicks, conversions per campaign and country), added up per country with CTR and CPA (spend / conversions). Countries are ranked by CPA (lowest first) when any country has conversions, else by CTR, and ranked_by says which; countries without that metric are listed last and are never called best or worst. Reports the data and the ranking only; it does not recommend budget moves. Give lookback_days (default 30, ending yesterday) or start_date and end_date, and optionally campaign_ids. Passes through truncated. Read-only.

**Platform:** TikTok Ads · **Type:** Read
