Build B2B campaign drafts with account discovery, organization selection, targeting estimates, creative checks, and PAUSED-only creation.

## Core features

- **Organization selection.** Read ad accounts and organization ACLs before campaign draft work begins.
- **Targeting and audience estimate.** Inspect targeting facets and block campaign creation when the estimated audience is below 300.
- **Creative validation.** Validate single image and carousel assets at 1200x627 or 1200x1200, with a 5MB file limit.
- **Paused campaign creation.** Create LinkedIn campaigns only in PAUSED status with a minimum 10 USD daily budget.

## Workflow

1. Check connection status and list LinkedIn ad accounts.
2. Select organization and campaign group context.
3. Review targeting facets and estimate audience size.
4. Validate creative asset specs and campaign draft guardrails.
5. Create only after review, with `execution_mode: "paused"`.

## Tools in that order

`get_linkedin_ad_accounts` and `get_linkedin_organizations` choose the account and the company page. `get_linkedin_campaign_groups` is the folder. `get_linkedin_campaigns` requires `account_id` and returns each campaign `id`, `name`, `status`, `account_id`, `campaign_group_id`, and `reference` (`urn:li:sponsoredCampaign:{id}`). `get_linkedin_creatives` requires `account_id`. Optional `campaign_id` limits the list to that campaign. Each creative has `id`, `campaign_id`, `status` (`intendedStatus`, then `status`), and `reference` (`urn:li:sponsoredCreative:{id}`). A missing account id returns unavailable. `get_linkedin_targeting_facets`, `get_linkedin_targeting_entities`, and `estimate_linkedin_audience` are reads. `validate_linkedin_creative_asset` and `validate_linkedin_campaign_draft` come before `create_linkedin_campaign`, which stays `PAUSED`.

`create_linkedin_creative` adds the ad after the campaign exists. Pause, enable, archive, delete, campaign groups, and creative status are on [LinkedIn groups and creatives](/ad-platforms/linkedin-groups). Those calls run on this request. `analyze_linkedin_wasted_spend` lists campaigns with spend and zero recorded conversions. It does not pause them.

`get_linkedin_budget_pricing` returns bid and budget recommendations. Applying a recommendation is a separate approval.
