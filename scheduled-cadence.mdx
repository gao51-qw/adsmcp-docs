A cadence delivers a read. It does not approve a budget, pause, or enable. Those still wait in [Approvals](/approvals).

## Three loops

| Loop | What you save | Where |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday scorecard | `schedule_brief` with cadence `weekly`. | [Reports and briefs](/reports) |
| Mid-month check | A second brief with cadence `monthly`, or a weekly brief whose instructions ask for pacing. | [Scheduled Briefs](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/scheduled-briefs) |
| CPA, ROAS, or CTR alert | `create_monitor` with the metric, operator, threshold, and lookback. | [Alerts](/alerts) |

`schedule_brief` stores cadence, timezone, recipients, platform scope, account scope, and output channels. Omitted cadence is `weekly`. Omitted timezone is `UTC`. Omitted platform scope is `meta`. Omitted output channel is `dashboard`. The dashboard form offers daily, weekly, and monthly. A recipient list on that form also sets the email channel. Call `list_scheduled_tasks` before creating another one.

An alert emails a prompt. `test_monitor` does not read live metrics. A forced test can stage a pending `pause_campaign`, and that pending action waits until a person approves or rejects it on the dashboard.

Automation rules are a fourth loop. A saved rule is already `active`. `test_automation_rule` compares the `metric_value` you pass and does not call the platform. See [Automation](/automation). Watch Agent is the separate admin scan. It is not this cadence.

## Daily morning check

The daily morning check looks at every ad platform you have connected each morning and tells you where money was spent without results. Turn it on with one click under **Daily morning check** at the top of [Scheduled Briefs](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/scheduled-briefs), or ask your AI client to call `schedule_brief` with `preset` `daily_check`:

```json
{ "preset": "daily_check", "timezone": "Europe/Berlin", "time": "08:00" }
```

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| `timezone` | Required. An IANA time zone such as `Europe/Berlin`. The dashboard uses your browser's. |
| `time` | Optional, `HH:MM` (24-hour). Default `08:00`. |
| Cadence | Daily. |
| Jobs | `checkup` (the account checkup of every ad platform), plus `negatives` (Google Ads negative-keyword candidates) when a Google Ads account is connected. |
| Platforms | Every ad platform you have connected when it runs (stored as `all`). Only connected, active accounts are read. |
| Delivery | The dashboard and your account email. |

You can have one daily check. Calling `schedule_brief` with the preset again updates it; a paused check stays paused until you resume it. Pass only `timezone`, `time` and `name` with the preset: anything it sets itself, such as `schedule_type`, `jobs`, `platforms`, `recipients` or `options`, is refused with `preset_argument_conflict`. Without `timezone` the call is refused with `timezone_required`.

Each digest starts with the checkup: per platform, the spend and the results of the last 7 complete days and their change against the 7 days before, and up to 5 campaigns that spent without results. A platform with an error, a reconnect, partial numbers or campaigns without a conversion goal gets one line. Amounts stay in each platform's own currency. Open it from the email's **Open the digest** link, from the card's **View the latest digest**, or with `get_latest_digest`. Like every brief, it is written by your own AI key from Settings, AI Providers, or sent as data with a prompt to paste into your AI client.

The daily check reads only. It creates nothing for approval itself. Its runs are not tool calls and do not count against your plan's calls. Pause or delete it on the card or with `manage_scheduled_task`.

The account checkup runs only in the daily check. Another brief with `checkup` in `jobs` is refused with `checkup_requires_daily_check`.

## What a run costs

Each tool call that runs counts as one call on the hosted plan. A scheduled brief's own run, the daily morning check included, is not a tool call; the calls your AI client makes afterwards are. Approving an item in the dashboard does not add a call. See [Pricing](/pricing).

A scorecard that calls `get_connections_status` and then `generate_cross_platform_report` is two calls before any extra platform read the agent adds. A monitor that only emails has not spent a write. Add the account id to the brief so the run does not scan every connected account.

Pause a schedule with `manage_scheduled_task` and action `pause` when the advertiser should drop out of the quota.
