The platform overview is [TikTok Ads](/ad-platforms/tiktok-ads); every tool is in the [API reference](/api-reference/introduction).

## Connect

Connect TikTok on [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections) (OAuth), then call `get_connections_status` and `get_tiktok_api_readiness`.

## Read and diagnose

- Structure: `get_tiktok_campaigns`, `get_tiktok_adgroups`, `get_tiktok_ads`.
- Performance: `get_tiktok_campaign_performance_report`, `get_tiktok_ad_performance`, `get_tiktok_performance_diagnostics`, `analyze_tiktok_geo_performance`.
- Creative: `get_tiktok_creative_analysis`, `detect_tiktok_creative_fatigue`, `validate_tiktok_video_asset`.
- Targeting: `search_tiktok_targeting_options`, `get_tiktok_interest_categories`, `get_tiktok_audience_insights`.

## Build and change

1. `validate_tiktok_campaign_draft`.
2. `create_tiktok_campaign` / `create_tiktok_video_campaign`, `create_tiktok_adgroup`, `create_tiktok_ad` (or Spark and carousel ads).
3. Pause or resume with the status tools; change a campaign with `update_tiktok_campaign`.

Every change is staged as a pending action; nothing happens in the ad account until a person approves it on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page. Campaigns are created paused and ad groups disabled. Enables and budget changes are checked against your [spend cap](/safety#spend-cap).
