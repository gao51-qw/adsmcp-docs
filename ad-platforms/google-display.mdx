---

Display is part of Google Ads, not a second connection. Finish Google OAuth first. See [Google Ads](/ad-platforms/google-ads).

## Order

1. `get_connections_status` and `get_google_api_readiness`.
2. `get_google_ads_accounts` and pick the client account.
3. `discover_google_existing_assets` if you want to reuse images already in the account.
4. `validate_google_display_assets`, then `validate_google_display_campaign_draft`.
5. `create_google_display_campaign` creates a paused campaign shell.
6. `create_google_display_ad` creates a paused responsive display ad.
7. Enabling the campaign is `enable_google_campaign`, and it waits in [Approvals](/approvals).

Targeting reads that often sit beside a Display draft are `resolve_google_locations`, `list_google_languages`, `search_google_audiences`, and `get_google_remarketing_lists`. Reading them does not attach them. A later write still needs approval.
