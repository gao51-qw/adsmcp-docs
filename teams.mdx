A team lets several people work on one paid plan. Each person keeps their own ADSMCP login, API keys and ad-account connections; their tool calls are charged to the team owner's pool. Manage it on [Team](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/team).

## Seats

| Owner's plan | Seats (owner included) |
| --- | --- |
| Free | 1 (cannot create a team) |
| Plus | 1 |
| Pro | 3 |
| Max | 10 |

- A pending invite that has not expired holds a seat.
- A person can belong to one team at a time.
- **Enterprise:** more seats are set up by ADSMCP on a contract. [Book a demo](https://adsmcp.si/book-demo).
- After a downgrade nobody is removed. Seats are counted owner first, then by join date; members beyond the new limit become **seat-inactive**: their tool calls are refused (`team_seat_inactive`, not counted) and they cannot approve, until the owner upgrades or frees a seat.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Billing (plan, overage switch, Lift my limit now), invite and remove anyone, change roles, set who may approve, delete the team. Approves. |
| Admin | Invite and remove members, revoke invites. Approves by default. No billing. |
| Member | Use tools on their own connections. Sees the team pool and their own usage. |

A member or admin who calls a billing action gets `403 team_owner_only`. The owner cannot leave the team; they delete it instead.

## Invites

1. The owner or an admin invites by email from the Team page. The invite link is valid for 7 days. It is emailed when email sending is configured; it can always be copied from the Team page.
2. The invitee signs in with the **same email address** the invite was sent to and accepts.
3. Someone who pays for their own Plus, Pro or Max plan cannot join (`has_own_subscription`): they cancel their own plan first, so nobody pays twice for one seat. Free users join directly.

When someone leaves or is removed, their own plan applies again.

## Shared quota

- Every member's tool calls are charged to the owner's pool: the same included calls, bonus calls, overage and yearly pool as the owner alone. See [Pricing](/pricing).
- Usage emails go to the owner.
- The billing page and `get_usage_status` show usage per member: owners and admins see everyone, members see their own.
- A member's API keys act as that member and are charged to the team pool. The number of keys a member may hold follows the team's plan. Read-only keys stay read-only.

## Ad accounts and the spend cap stay per person

- Each member connects their own ad accounts. A member's tools only ever use that member's connections; there is no shared connection.
- The [active-account cap](/accounts#active-accounts) of each member follows the team's plan and is re-applied when someone joins, leaves or is removed, when the team is deleted and when the owner's plan changes.
- The [spend cap](/safety#spend-cap) checked when a change runs is the one of the person who staged it.

## Approvals in a team

- By default the owner and admins can approve. The owner can limit it to the owner only, or let members approve **their own** staged changes.
- An approver can approve a pending action staged by any team member. It still runs as its **author**: the author's connection, active accounts and spend cap.
- The author can always reject their own action.
- An action whose result contains a one-time secret (`create_chatgpt_conversions_api_key`) can only be approved by its author.
- Approval stays dashboard-only, as for everyone: over MCP and REST an AI client can view and reject, never approve. Every approval decision is recorded with who decided, their role, the author and the tool.

See [Approvals](/approvals).

## Deleting a team

The owner deletes the team on the Team page. Members and invites are removed and everyone returns to their own plan.
