This guide covers what you can automate on Meta ads through ADSMCP. The platform overview is [Meta Ads](/ad-platforms/meta-ads).

## 1. Connect and check

Connect Meta on [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections) (OAuth). Then ask your agent to call `get_connections_status` and `get_ad_accounts`.

## 2. Automate the reading

- Performance: `get_account_insights`, `get_campaign_insights`, `get_ad_insights`, and the placement, demographic and creative breakdowns.
- Audits: `audit_entire_account`, `diagnose_campaign_health`, `detect_creative_fatigue`.
- Recurring delivery: `schedule_brief` ([Reports and briefs](/reports)) or a CPA/ROAS/CTR [alert](/alerts).

## 3. Stage the changes

- Pause or enable: `pause_campaign`, `enable_campaign`, `pause_ad_set`, `enable_ad_set`.
- Budgets: `update_campaign_budget` (minor currency units by default).
- New campaigns: `create_campaign`, `create_ad_set`, `create_ad`, or the one-call `create_meta_image_campaign`, `create_meta_video_campaign` and `create_meta_carousel_campaign` (traffic, awareness and engagement objectives). All are created paused.
- Budget suggestions: `optimize_budget` stages its recommended budget changes for approval.

Every change is staged as a pending action; nothing happens in the ad account until a person approves it on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page. Budget changes and enables are checked against your [spend cap](/safety#spend-cap).

## 4. What ADSMCP will not do

- Replace Meta's own automated rules or Advantage+ automation.
- Apply a change without a person's approval, or retry a failed one.
