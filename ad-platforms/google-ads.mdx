ADSMCP connects your AI client to Google Ads: accounts, campaigns, ad groups, ads, keywords, assets, audiences and conversions. Your own AI model does the reasoning; ADSMCP returns the data and stages the changes.

## Connect

Connect Google Ads on [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections) (OAuth, `adwords` scope; Google may ask for a [passkey](/knowledge-base/google-passkey)). Then call `get_connections_status`, `get_google_api_readiness` and `get_google_ads_accounts`.

- Pass the customer id as `account_id` (digits, as a string). Add `login_customer_id` when you reach a client account through a manager account.
- Campaign creates take `budget_micros` (1,000,000 micros = 1 unit of the account currency). `update_google_campaign_budget` takes `budget_amount` in normal units.

## What you can do

- **Report:** `get_google_ads_performance_report`, ad group / ad / keyword performance, budgets and bid strategies.
- **Analyze:** `analyze_search_terms` and `analyze_wasted_spend` score the rows you pass in; `explain_google_performance_anomaly` and `optimize_google_budget_allocation` work from account data.
- **Plan:** keyword ideas (`get_google_keyword_ideas`: keyword, monthly searches, competition), search theme ideas, locations, languages and audiences.
- **Validate:** Search, Display, Performance Max and YouTube / Demand Gen drafts and assets.
- **Change (approval):** create Search, Performance Max, Display, YouTube, Demand Gen, Shopping and App campaigns (paused); ad groups, responsive search / display / video ads, keywords and negatives, negative keyword lists, sitelinks / callouts / calls / snippets, audiences and conversion actions; pause, enable, budget, bidding and keyword bid changes.

Not available yet: editing PMax audience signals or search themes, removing keywords, and Google Ads features not listed below. See **Not supported yet** in the [API reference](/api-reference/introduction).

## Changes need your approval

Every change below is staged as a pending action. Nothing changes in the account until a person approves it on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page; your AI client can view or reject it, never approve it. When an approved change runs, new objects are created paused or as drafts, budget changes and enables are checked against your [spend cap](/safety#spend-cap), and a failure is not retried. `remove_*` / `delete_*` tools also need `confirm_delete: true`.
