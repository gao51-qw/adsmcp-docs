## Accounts

- One ADSMCP login can connect several ad platforms. Plans allow one active ad account per platform (Free, Plus, Pro) or up to five per platform (Max). See [Pricing](/pricing) and [Multiple accounts](/accounts).
- Google manager accounts and Amazon profiles are chosen per call with the account id.
- For strict separation between clients, use a separate ADSMCP account per client.

## Approvals

Every change is staged as a pending action; nothing happens in the ad account until a person approves it on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page. Assign the people who approve. The AI agent — yours or a client's — can only propose, view and reject.

## Reporting

- `generate_cross_platform_report`, `export_report_csv` and `export_report_google_sheets` build client reports.
- `schedule_brief` sends recurring briefs; a shared report link at `/r/{token}` shows a report without a login ([Public reports](/public-reports)).

## Guardrails per client

Set each client's targets in Brand / Business Context and the Max Daily Budget [spend cap](/safety#spend-cap). Keep each client's account ids in the agent's project instructions so it never guesses.

## What ADSMCP does not include

Per-seat team billing or an AI model. Your team's AI clients use your own models.
