title: "Claude Cowork"
description: "Use ADSMCP in Claude Cowork through the same custom connector as Claude."
---

Cowork uses the connectors on your Claude account. Add ADSMCP once as described on [Claude](/ai-clients/claude) (custom connector, URL `https://mcp.adsmcp.si/mcp`, OAuth sign-in), then enable it for the Cowork task.

There is no separate Cowork plugin for ADSMCP in Anthropic's directory. Scheduled or long-running Cowork tasks follow the same rule as every client: they can read and propose, and every change waits for a person on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page. A Cowork task cannot apply a change by itself.

Put your account ids, KPI targets and the line "every change goes to ADSMCP Approvals" in the task instructions. See [Set up a project](/set-up-a-project).

## After you connect

1. Ask: *"Call get_connections_status and tell me which ad platforms are connected. Do not change anything."*
2. If a platform is missing, connect it on [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections).
3. Ask for a read, such as last week's performance, before asking for changes.

Every change the client proposes is staged as a pending action. Approve or reject it on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page; the client cannot approve it. The client runs on your own AI model and plan, and each ADSMCP tool call counts against your [ADSMCP plan](/pricing).
