One ADSMCP login can hold connections for Google, Meta, Amazon, TikTok, LinkedIn, ChatGPT Ads and Microsoft Ads at the same time, plus Google Analytics 4, Klaviyo, [Search Console](/marketing-stack/google-search-console) and [Tag Manager](/marketing-stack/google-tag-manager) for reporting. `get_connections_status` reports those platforms. `list_connected_accounts` groups the accounts. Platform tools then take the account id you name. Pass it as a string.

The agent must not pick an account you did not name when several are connected. Ask it to list them and wait.

## Active accounts

Connections are unlimited. Only **active** accounts can be used by tools, and your plan sets how many can be active on each ad platform:

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 1 | — |
| Plus | 1 | 5 |
| Pro | 1 | 5 |
| Max | 5 | 5 |

- The cap is per platform (Google Ads, Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Amazon, Microsoft Ads, ChatGPT Ads). Google Analytics 4, Klaviyo, Search Console and Tag Manager are not capped.
- The first time you connect a platform, the most recently connected accounts up to the cap become active.
- Choose which accounts are active in the **Active accounts** card on [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections). Your choice is kept when you reconnect.
- After a downgrade, the most recently connected accounts stay active and the rest become inactive automatically. Nothing is disconnected.
- A tool call that names an inactive account is refused with `403 ad_account_inactive` and is not counted against your quota. Activate the account, or name an active one.

More than five active accounts per platform, or several people on one plan: see [Teams](/teams) or [book a demo](https://adsmcp.si/book-demo).

## Manager accounts and profiles

Google manager accounts still appear through `get_google_ads_accounts`. Amazon profiles appear through `get_amazon_profiles`. ChatGPT Ads accounts appear through `list_chatgpt_accounts`. Choosing a client account under a manager, or a profile under a seller, is that id argument. It is not a second ADSMCP login.

## More than one AI client

ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor and the other clients on [AI clients](/ai-clients) share your account. They do not each get a fresh quota. A token you create for one client spends the same calls as a token you create for another. Revoke the token that should stop.

## Team members

In a [team](/teams) each member signs in with their own ADSMCP account and connects their own ad accounts; a member's tools only ever use that member's connections. The active-account cap of each member follows the team's plan. Tool calls are charged to the team owner's pool.
