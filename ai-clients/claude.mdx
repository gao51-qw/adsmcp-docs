---

ADSMCP is not in Claude's connector directory. Add it as a custom connector:

1. In Claude, open **Settings → Connectors** and choose **Add custom connector**.
2. Name it `ADSMCP` and enter the URL `https://mcp.adsmcp.si/mcp`.
3. Choose **Connect**. A browser window opens: sign in to ADSMCP and choose **Allow**.
4. In a chat, enable the ADSMCP connector from the tools menu.

No API key is needed: Claude uses OAuth. The connector is shared by claude.ai, Claude Desktop and the mobile apps on the same Claude account. Custom connectors depend on your Claude plan and, for organizations, on the owner allowing them.

For slash commands and the performance-marketing agent, use [Claude Code](/ai-clients/claude-code) with the `adsmcp` plugin.

## After you connect

1. Ask: *"Call get_connections_status and tell me which ad platforms are connected. Do not change anything."*
2. If a platform is missing, connect it on [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections).
3. Ask for a read, such as last week's performance, before asking for changes.

Every change the client proposes is staged as a pending action. Approve or reject it on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page; the client cannot approve it. The client runs on your own AI model and plan, and each ADSMCP tool call counts against your [ADSMCP plan](/pricing).
