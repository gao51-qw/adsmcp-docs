ADSMCP is not in ChatGPT's app or plugin directory. Add it as a custom connector:

1. In ChatGPT settings, turn on **developer mode** for apps and connectors.
2. Create a connector named `ADSMCP` with the MCP server URL `https://mcp.adsmcp.si/mcp`.
3. Choose **OAuth** as the authentication and finish the ADSMCP sign-in (**Allow**).
4. In a chat, enable the ADSMCP connector.

Custom connectors depend on your ChatGPT plan and workspace settings.

ChatGPT as an AI client is separate from **ChatGPT Ads** as an ad platform. To manage ChatGPT Ads campaigns, connect the ChatGPT Ads account on [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections); see [ChatGPT Ads](/ad-platforms/chatgpt-ads).

## After you connect

1. Ask: *"Call get_connections_status and tell me which ad platforms are connected. Do not change anything."*
2. If a platform is missing, connect it on [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections).
3. Ask for a read, such as last week's performance, before asking for changes.

Every change the client proposes is staged as a pending action. Approve or reject it on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page; the client cannot approve it. The client runs on your own AI model and plan, and each ADSMCP tool call counts against your [ADSMCP plan](/pricing).
