title: "Perplexity"
description: "Add the ADSMCP MCP URL in Perplexity and keep spend changes behind approval."
---

The marketing page is [Connect Perplexity](https://adsmcp.si/perplexity). In Perplexity MCP settings, add `https://mcp.adsmcp.si/mcp` and finish OAuth.

Perplexity can research a market and then read a connected account. The account read still requires [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections). A research answer is not a live budget change.

First prompt: "Check my connected ad platforms." Optimization plans stage [Pending Actions](/approvals). There is no separate Perplexity plugin.
