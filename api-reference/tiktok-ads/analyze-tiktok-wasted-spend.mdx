Campaigns that spent without return: by ROAS (losing_money under 1.0, below_target under target_roas) when TikTok reports conversion value, else by cost per conversion (no_conversions, high_cpa). Each flagged campaign has wasted spend, severity (CRITICAL / HIGH / MEDIUM by share of account spend), delivery status, learning phase, action_options codes (a campaign with conversions never gets pause; one in learning gets wait_for_learning) and, with include_fatigue, ads whose hook rate fell. Read-only; findings are data.

**Platform:** TikTok Ads · **Type:** Read
