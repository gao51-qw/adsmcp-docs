---
title: "Google Shopping"
description: "Create a paused Google Shopping campaign with Merchant Center settings."
---

Shopping uses the Google Ads connection and a Merchant Center link on the draft. See [Google Ads](/ad-platforms/google-ads).

## Order

1. `get_connections_status` and `get_google_api_readiness`.
2. `get_google_ads_accounts` and confirm the client account that owns the Merchant Center link.
3. `create_google_shopping_campaign` creates a paused Shopping campaign with the Merchant Center settings on the draft.
4. Enabling the campaign is `enable_google_campaign` and waits in [Approvals](/approvals).

There is no Shopping validation playbook. The create itself stays paused. Product ads and listing groups are not created by that one call.
