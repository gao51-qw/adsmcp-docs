This guide covers what you can automate on Google Ads through ADSMCP. Tool details are in the [Google Ads API reference](/api-reference/introduction); the platform overview is [Google Ads](/ad-platforms/google-ads).

## 1. Connect and check

Connect Google Ads on [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections) (OAuth, `adwords` scope). Then ask your agent to call `get_connections_status` and `get_google_ads_accounts`.

## 2. Automate the reading

- Weekly performance: `get_google_ads_performance_report`, ad group / ad / keyword performance tools.
- Search terms and waste: `analyze_search_terms` and `analyze_wasted_spend` score the rows you pass in; pair them with the performance reads.
- Anomalies and budget split: `explain_google_performance_anomaly`, `optimize_google_budget_allocation`.
- Schedule the report with `schedule_brief` ([Reports and briefs](/reports)), or set a CPA/ROAS [alert](/alerts).

## 3. Stage the changes

Keyword pauses and bids (`pause_google_keyword`, `update_google_keyword_bid`), negative keywords (`add_google_negative_keywords`), budgets (`update_google_campaign_budget`), bidding (`update_google_campaign_bidding`), assets and new campaigns (`create_search_campaign` and the other create tools).

Every change is staged as a pending action; nothing happens in the ad account until a person approves it on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page. Budget changes and enables are checked against your [spend cap](/safety#spend-cap); new campaigns are created paused.

## 4. What ADSMCP will not do

- Approve or apply changes on a schedule by itself.
- Retry a failed change automatically.
- Run Google Ads scripts or automated rules inside Google Ads; those stay in Google Ads.
