Copy a Google Ads Search campaign into a Microsoft Ads account as a new campaign, sent Paused, with its ad groups, keywords, negative keywords, responsive search ads, sitelinks, callouts and mapped location targets (preview_google_to_microsoft_copy shows the plan and what is not copied). Ad groups, keywords and ads keep their Google status (enabled -\> Active, paused -\> Paused; removed ones are not copied): nothing serves while the campaign is paused, and enabling it later is a separate approved change that goes through the spend cap. Without location_map entries no location target is copied and the new campaign targets all locations; the approval says so. The plan is read from Google when the call is staged and shown in the approval with its counts and not_copied list. On approval ADSMCP creates it in order (campaign, locations, ad groups, keywords, negatives, ads, extensions) and reports each step; if a step fails it stops and reports what exists (nothing is deleted). A replay never creates a second campaign. The daily budget is checked against the spend cap. Staged for dashboard approval; nothing changes in Microsoft Advertising before a person approves it.

**Platform:** Microsoft Ads · **Type:** Change
