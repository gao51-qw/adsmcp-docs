YouTube ads run through the Google Ads connection. There is no separate YouTube OAuth step in ADSMCP. See [Google Ads](/ad-platforms/google-ads).

## Order

1. `get_connections_status` and `get_google_ads_accounts`.
2. `validate_youtube_video_asset` checks the video metadata.
3. `validate_google_youtube_demand_gen_draft` with `campaign_type: "youtube"` checks the campaign draft.
4. `create_google_youtube_campaign` creates a paused campaign shell.
5. `create_google_video_ad` creates a paused video ad after approval.
6. `enable_google_campaign` is a separate approved action. `verify_google_campaign_is_live` only reports status.

Demand Gen uses the same draft validator and its own paused create, `create_google_demand_gen_campaign`. A Demand Gen campaign is not a YouTube-only shell. See [Google Demand Gen](/ad-platforms/google-demand-gen).

## Video check

`validate_youtube_video_asset` returns `status: "valid"` or `status: "blocked"`, plus `errors`, `warnings`, `requirements`, and `source` `services-mcp-google-validation`. It does not upload the video.

| Check | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| `video_url` | HTTPS URL containing `youtu.be` or `youtube.com`. |
| `visibility` | `public` or `unlisted`. |
| `duration_seconds` | At least 10. |
| `headline` | Required, 30 characters maximum. |
| `description` | 90 characters maximum. |
| `business_name` | 25 characters maximum. |
| `logo` | Omitted logo adds a warning. It does not block. |

The YouTube draft validator also requires `account_id`, `campaign_type: "youtube"`, an HTTPS `final_url`, and `daily_budget` of at least 10. Schema-required fields are `account_id`, `campaign_type`, `final_url`, and `daily_budget`. When `campaign_type` is `youtube`, that draft also runs the video-asset checks and copies those errors and warnings. A missing video URL, visibility, duration, or headline blocks the draft.

## Campaign create

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| `account_id`, `name`, `budget_micros` | Required. `budget_micros` must be a positive integer. |
| `target_cpa_micros` | Optional. When positive, it is sent as the maximize-conversions target CPA. |
| `execution_mode` | `approval_required` returns `status: "approval_required"` and does not call Google on that request. |
| `validate_only` | Forwarded on the Google mutate. |
| `login_customer_id` | Manager account id when Google requires one. |

The campaign status is `PAUSED` and the channel is `VIDEO`. This call does not attach a video. A direct create returns `created_paused` when the adapter returns a campaign resource name or campaign id. Missing `GOOGLE_DEVELOPER_TOKEN` returns `blocked` / `google_adapter_unavailable`. A create that does not return a campaign id returns `blocked` / `no_connection`. The same return shape is on [Google Shopping](/ad-platforms/google-shopping).

## Video ad after approval

`execution_mode: "approval_required"` on `create_google_video_ad` stages the call locally and does not call Google. A direct call with any other mode, including an omitted mode, has no local create branch and is forwarded upstream.

The approved executor calls the adapter, which writes the ad with status `PAUSED`. The adapter requires `account_id`, `ad_group_id`, `video_id`, and at least one headline. Headlines are capped at 5 and cut to 15 characters. The first headline is also sent as one long headline cut to 90 characters. Descriptions are capped at 2 and cut to 35 characters. `call_to_action` is cut to 15 characters when present. Optional `final_urls` are sent when non-empty. `video_id` is sent as a Google Ads asset resource, not as the watch URL from the validator. The published input schema is permissive.
