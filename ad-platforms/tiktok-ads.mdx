ADSMCP connects your AI client to TikTok Ads: campaigns, ad groups, ads, audiences, pixels, videos and targeting options.

## Connect

Connect TikTok on [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections) (OAuth). Then call `get_connections_status` and `get_tiktok_api_readiness`. Pass the `advertiser_id` as a string.

## What you can do

- **Report:** campaign and ad performance, creative analysis, geo performance, performance diagnostics, audience insights.
- **Plan:** targeting options, regions, languages, interest categories and keywords, bid recommendations, Spark identities, existing assets.
- **Validate:** campaign drafts and video assets.
- **Change (approval):** create campaigns (paused), ad groups (disabled), ads, Spark ads, carousel ads and video campaigns; upload videos and images; custom and lookalike audiences; pause / resume / status changes; campaign name and budget updates.

## Changes need your approval

Every change below is staged as a pending action. Nothing changes in the account until a person approves it on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page; your AI client can view or reject it, never approve it. When an approved change runs, new objects are created paused or as drafts, budget changes and enables are checked against your [spend cap](/safety#spend-cap), and a failure is not retried. `remove_*` / `delete_*` tools also need `confirm_delete: true`.
