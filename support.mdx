ADSMCP support is answered by people, not a bot.

## Support chat

Open [Support Chat](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/support), or the chat button at the bottom right of any dashboard page.

- Write your question and attach screenshots: up to **5 PNG or JPEG images per message, 5 MB each** (paste, drag and drop, or the paperclip). Messages can be up to 5,000 characters.
- Tick the diagnostics box to attach a snapshot of your plan, your connected platforms and their status, and up to 10 recent error codes from the last 7 days. Nothing is attached unless you tick it, and no free text, tokens or ids are copied.
- A reply appears in the chat and is emailed to your account address. The page refreshes while it is open.
- Your conversation continues where you left off for **7 days** after the last message; after that a new message starts a new conversation, and earlier ones stay readable.
- Limits: 10 messages per 10 minutes, 60 messages and 30 screenshots a day, 5 new conversations a day.

Before you send, remove secrets. Text that looks like an API key or a platform token (`sk_live_`, `pb_`, `Bearer …`, Google, Meta and other common token formats) is replaced with `[redacted]`, but do not rely on it. Screenshots are stored in private storage that only you and ADSMCP support can open.

## Email

- Product and documentation questions: [support@adsmcp.si](mailto:support@adsmcp.si).
- Billing questions: [sales@adsmcp.si](mailto:sales@adsmcp.si).

## Agencies and teams: Book a demo

For several people on one plan, more seats than your plan includes, or many client accounts, use [Book a demo](https://adsmcp.si/book-demo). The form asks for your name, work email, company, team size, monthly ad spend and an optional message, and goes to the sales team. See also [Teams](/teams).

## Include this

- The time of the call, in UTC if you have it.
- The tool name, such as `get_connections_status` or `create_search_campaign`.
- The HTTP status or MCP error code, and its `reason` or `detail`.
- The platform, and whether [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections) still shows it connected.
- What you expected to happen.

## Leave this out

Do not send the bearer token, a platform refresh token, a card number, or an export of customer data. A redacted error is enough: `get_recent_errors` and `get_request_trace` return your own recent errors. Admins can also copy one from [Diagnostics](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/diagnostics).

Check [FAQ](/faq) and [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting) before writing. Connection, approval and quota questions are answered there.
