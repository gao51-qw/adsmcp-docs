## What is ADSMCP?

An MCP server and dashboard that gives your AI client ad-account data, analysis tools and safe change proposals for Google, Meta, Amazon, TikTok, LinkedIn and ChatGPT Ads. The AI model is always your own; ADSMCP does not provide one.

## Where is the MCP server?

`https://mcp.adsmcp.si/mcp`. The dashboard is [https://adsmcp.si](https://adsmcp.si). Docs search for coding agents is `https://docs.adsmcp.si/mcp`, which cannot call ad tools.

## Why did the client connect but the account is empty?

Signing in the AI client and connecting an ad account are different steps. Open [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections), connect the platform, then call `get_connections_status`.

## Can my AI agent change my campaigns?

It can propose changes. Every change (create, pause, enable, budget, bid, delete) is staged as a pending action. Only a person approves it, on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page. The agent can list and reject pending actions but cannot approve them, in chat or over the API.

## Why is the new campaign not spending?

New campaigns are created paused. Turning one on is a separate change that also needs your approval on the dashboard, and is checked against your [spend cap](/safety#spend-cap).

## A change failed. Will it retry?

No. A change that fails when it runs is not retried automatically. It shows on the dashboard [Execution Queue](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/execution-queue). Fix the cause and stage a corrected change. See [Approvals](/approvals).

## How do I delete something?

`remove_*` and `delete_*` tools need `confirm_delete: true`. The delete is then staged and still needs approval on the dashboard. Once approved it cannot be undone.

## What happens at the quota limit?

The call is refused with `quota_exceeded`. `get_usage_status` shows your plan's usage. Changes are also blocked when the quota check cannot be reached. Limits are on [Pricing](/pricing).

## Which clients have slash commands?

Claude Code (via the `adsmcp` plugin or the installer), Codex and OpenClaw get `/adsmcp:setup`, `/adsmcp:performance-review`, `/adsmcp:write-ad-copy`, `/adsmcp:wasted-spend` and `/adsmcp:campaign`. Other clients use the same steps in plain language. See [Workflows](/workflows).

## Which account will the agent use?

If more than one account is connected, name the account id. `list_connected_accounts` lists them. See [Multiple accounts](/accounts).

## Can I use ChatGPT Ads?

Yes, for reading performance and for pausing, resuming and changing budgets or bids of existing campaigns, ad groups and ads (with approval). Creating ChatGPT Ads campaigns is not supported yet. See [ChatGPT Ads](/ad-platforms/chatgpt-ads). ChatGPT can also be the AI client that calls ADSMCP.

## Does ADSMCP use its own AI model?

No. Your AI client (Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor, …) does the reasoning and writing with your own model and account. ADSMCP returns data and computed analysis.

## The call failed. Where do I look?

Use [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting). Match `invalid_token`, `quota_exceeded`, `approval_requires_dashboard`, `spend_cap_exceeded` or the platform's error before you try again.
