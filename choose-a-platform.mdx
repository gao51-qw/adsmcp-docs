title: "Choose an ad platform"

description: "Pick Meta, Google, TikTok, LinkedIn, or Amazon from the work ADSMCP can do on that platform."
---

Connect a platform on [Connections](/connections) before asking an agent to read it. `get_connections_status` names what is connected. When several accounts are connected, pass the account id you mean. See [Multiple accounts](/accounts).

| Platform | Use it when the job is | Start here |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Ads | Search, Performance Max, Shopping, Display, YouTube, Demand Gen, a paused App campaign shell, keywords, or assets. | [Google Ads](/ad-platforms/google-ads) |
| Meta Ads | Facebook and Instagram campaigns, lead forms, catalog ads, pixels, or experiments. | [Meta Ads](/ad-platforms/meta-ads) |
| TikTok Ads | In-feed video, Spark Ads, carousels, targeting lists, or short-form performance. | [TikTok Ads](/ad-platforms/tiktok-ads) |
| LinkedIn Ads | A company-page campaign, job or industry targeting, or an audience estimate. | [LinkedIn Ads](/ad-platforms/linkedin-ads) |
| Amazon Ads | Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, or Sponsored Display on a seller or vendor profile. | [Amazon Ads](/ad-platforms/amazon-ads) |

Google Analytics 4 explains on-site sessions and conversions. Klaviyo reads email lists, segments, and flows, and can create one campaign draft. Neither one is an ad account. See [Google Analytics 4](/marketing-stack/google-analytics) and [Klaviyo](/marketing-stack/klaviyo).

Creates on these platforms stay paused or wait for approval, with the exceptions each platform page names. A recommendation from a read does not change the live budget.
