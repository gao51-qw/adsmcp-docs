List your shared dashboards (active, expired, revoked) with their accounts, blocks and expiry, your effective plan's limits (the team plan for a team member; scope: team counts the team's active links together), and the connected accounts a new dashboard can use (active: false marks an account inactive on your plan, which cannot be used). Links are never listed: they are shown once, at creation.
