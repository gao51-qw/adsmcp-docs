ADSMCP connects your AI client to LinkedIn Ads: ad accounts, organizations, campaign groups, campaigns, creatives, lead forms and analytics.

## Connect

Connect LinkedIn on [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections) (OAuth; re-authorize if ad-account scopes are missing). Then call `get_connections_status` and `get_linkedin_ad_accounts`. Pass the `account_id` as a string.

## What you can do

- **Report:** campaign analytics, engagement metrics, creative performance, audience insights, anomaly explanations, budget suggestions and supply forecasts.
- **Plan:** targeting facets and entities, similar entities, audience size estimates and budget pricing.
- **Validate:** campaign drafts and creative assets.
- **Change (approval):** create campaign groups and campaigns (paused) and creatives (`DRAFT`, no media upload); pause, enable, archive and delete campaigns; change budgets, names, targeting, audience expansion and schedules; change creative and group status.

Not available yet: conversions, cloning, batch updates and per-format campaign builders.

## Changes need your approval

Every change below is staged as a pending action. Nothing changes in the account until a person approves it on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page; your AI client can view or reject it, never approve it. When an approved change runs, new objects are created paused or as drafts, budget changes and enables are checked against your [spend cap](/safety#spend-cap), and a failure is not retried. `remove_*` / `delete_*` tools also need `confirm_delete: true`.
