## Prerequisites

> [!NOTE]
>
> * A Claude **Max**, **Team**, or **Enterprise** subscription (Cowork requires Max or above)
> * An ADSMCP account ([free to start](https://adsmcp.si))
> * At least one ad platform account: Google Ads, Meta Ads, Amazon Ads, ChatGPT Ads, LinkedIn Ads, or TikTok Ads

## Setup (5 Minutes)

Search for ADSMCP under **Customize → Plugins** and click **Add**. One install brings in ADSMCP's Anthropic-verified connector, 15 skills, 5 slash commands, and a Performance Marketing Agent.

### 1. Open Cowork and Customize

**1. Open Claude Cowork**

Open the Claude desktop app (or claude.ai) and click the **Cowork** tab at the top.

![Claude Cowork tab](/images/0GXeYWLZAEKikC6R/images/claude-cowork/cowork-tab.png)

Select the Cowork tab in Claude

**2. Go to Customize**

Click **Customize** in the left sidebar. Skills, Connectors, and Plugins are all managed here.

### 2. Add the ADSMCP plugin from Anthropic's directory

The plugin comes from Anthropic's directory and bundles the verified **ADSMCP Ads & Performance Marketing Agent** connector with 15 skills, 5 slash commands, and a Performance Marketing Agent. You don't add a custom connector or paste a URL.

**1. Search Plugins for ADSMCP**

In **Customize**, open **Plugins**, select **Discover**, and search for **adsmcp**. Choose **ADSMCP** — the listing reads **from Anthropic Directory**. Or open the [ADSMCP plugin](https://claude.ai/customize/plugins/id/7bb5ea42-d7d7-4256-89d7-5798f5dc9c83%40anthropic-plugin-directory) directly.

![ADSMCP plugin listing in Claude, from Anthropic Directory, with tabs for Skills 15, Commands 5, Connectors 1, and Agents 1 and an Add button](/images/PuyhsbFPuRU-UDud/images/claude/plugin-directory.png)

The ADSMCP plugin in Anthropic's directory: 15 skills, 5 commands, 1 connector, 1 agent

**2. Click Add**

Click **Add**. The **Contents** tab lists what installs:

* **Commands**: `/setup`, `/performance-review`, `/write-ad-copy`, `/wasted-spend`, `/refresh-brand-context`
* **Skills**: 15 skills that teach Claude ADSMCP's advertising workflows, per platform and per task
* **Connector**: **ADSMCP Ads & Performance Marketing Agent**, the Anthropic-verified connector with 400+ tools
* **Agent**: a Performance Marketing Agent that runs ad work with your brand context

**3. Connect ADSMCP**

Claude asks you to connect **ADSMCP Ads & Performance Marketing Agent**. Click **Connect**. You can also do it later from **Customize → Connectors**.

> [!WARNING]
>
> **Connect button grayed out?** Two common causes:
>
> * **You're on a VPN.** Many corporate VPNs block the handshake to `mcp.adsmcp.si`. Disconnect from the VPN and try again.
> * **Your workspace hasn't allowed it.** On Claude Team or Enterprise, an owner controls which directory connectors and plugins members can use. Ask them to allow ADSMCP. It's an Anthropic-verified listing, so there's no custom URL for them to approve.

**4. Sign in to ADSMCP**

Your browser opens ADSMCP's sign-in page. Sign in (or create an account), approve Claude, and link your Google Ads, Meta Ads, Amazon Ads, ChatGPT Ads, LinkedIn Ads, and TikTok accounts at [adsmcp.ai/connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections).

Once you've approved it, the connector shows as connected and its tools are listed under tool permissions.

> [!TIP]
>
> **Recommended tool permissions.** Set **Interactive** and **Read-only** tools to **Always allow** so research, reporting, and dashboards run without interruption. Leave **Write / delete** tools on **Needs approval**, so anything that changes a campaign still pauses for your confirmation. ADSMCP also creates every new campaign paused by default.

> [!TIP]
>
> If the connector doesn't show as connected right away, restart Claude and check again.

### 3. Open a brand folder and try it

**1. Work in a Brand Folder**

Back on the Cowork main screen, click **Work in a folder** and select the folder containing your brand documents — voice of customer research, brand guidelines, previous campaigns, keyword research, audience targeting docs, or any other brand context.

Claude Cowork will use this folder as your brand workspace. The more context you provide, the better ADSMCP's recommendations and ad copy will be.

> [!NOTE]
>
> Your brand folder can contain docs or be completely empty — the agent builds context from your live ad platform data either way. More docs = better brand context = better ad copy and recommendations.

**2. Try It Now**

> **[Sign up for ADSMCP — free to start](https://adsmcp.si)**
>
> 15 free tool calls/month. No credit card required. Connect your ad accounts in 2 minutes.

> [!TIP]

## FAQ

> **What is Claude Cowork?**
>
> Claude Cowork is a **workspace mode inside the Claude desktop app** (also on claude.ai) where you point Claude at a folder on your machine and it reads everything inside as context — brand docs, voice guidelines, past campaigns, keyword research. You then chat with Claude about that work, and the agent has full visibility into your brand. Cowork is built around folders + plugins + connectors. It's where brand-aware advertising work happens.

> **Is Cowork the same thing as Claude Code?**
>
> **No — they are two different products from Anthropic.**
>
> * **Claude Cowork** is the workspace mode inside the Claude desktop app and claude.ai. You use it through a GUI. It's the page you're on right now.
> * **[Claude Code](/ai-clients/claude-code)** is Anthropic's terminal/CLI developer tool. You run `claude` in a shell, it works inside your codebase, and it's optimized for software-engineering workflows. It also has a desktop / IDE-side surface.
>
> They're separate apps. Cowork ≠ Claude Code. If you typed `claude` in your terminal to get here, you want the [Claude Code setup page](/ai-clients/claude-code), not this one.

> **How is Cowork different from Claude Chat?**
>
> Claude Chat (the standard chat interface) supports connectors — you add an MCP server URL and that's it. Cowork supports the same connectors **plus** plugins (slash commands, skills, agents) **plus** brand folders. For one-off ad questions, Chat is fine. For ongoing brand-aware work, Cowork is the richer surface.

> **Which Claude plans support Cowork?**
>
> Cowork is available on Claude Max, Team, and Enterprise plans. Pro plans have access to Chat connectors but not the full Cowork experience.

> **What does the ADSMCP plugin include?**
>
> The plugin installs ADSMCP's Anthropic-verified connector (**ADSMCP Ads & Performance Marketing Agent**), 5 slash commands (setup, performance-review, write-ad-copy, wasted-spend, refresh-brand-context), 15 advertising skills, and 1 performance marketing agent. It all comes in one **Customize → Plugins → Add** step.

> **The Add or Connect button is grayed out — why?**
>
> Two common causes:
>
> * **You're on a VPN.** Many corporate VPNs block the handshake to `mcp.adsmcp.si`. Disconnect from the VPN and the button becomes clickable.
> * **Your workspace owner hasn't allowed it.** On Claude Team or Enterprise, owners decide which directory connectors and plugins members can use. Ask your owner or IT admin to allow **ADSMCP Ads & Performance Marketing Agent**. It's an Anthropic-verified directory listing, so there's no custom connector for them to review.
>
> Once either is resolved, the button activates and you can continue.

> **Claude says "No OAuth flow is in progress"**
>
> This is a known **client-side** bug in the Claude desktop/Cowork app — Claude loses its sign-in session between steps and shows *"No OAuth flow is in progress."* It's an issue in Anthropic's app, not a problem with your ADSMCP account or connection.
>
> **Fix:** go to **Customize → Connectors**, open **ADSMCP Ads & Performance Marketing Agent**, and click **Connect** from there instead of from the plugin prompt. If it still fails, disconnect it, fully quit and reopen Claude, and connect again. Don't add ADSMCP as a custom connector. The verified listing is the same server, and a second copy duplicates every tool.

> **Can I use both Cowork and Claude Chat?**
>
> Yes. The Cowork plugin/connector and Claude Chat connector work independently. You can use Cowork for brand-aware workspace sessions and Chat for quick ad questions. Both connect to the same ADSMCP MCP server and share one tool-call quota.

> **How much does this cost?**
>
> ADSMCP pricing is based on tool calls, not ad spend. Free tier: 15 calls/month. Plus: $49/mo for 150 calls. Pro: $99/mo for 600 calls. Max: \$199/mo for 3,000 calls. See [full pricing](https://adsmcp.si/pricing).
