If your organisation is a registered non-profit, NGO or charity, you can get a discount on the **first year** of a yearly plan. Ask in the support chat at [adsmcp.ai](https://adsmcp.si) and you get a discount code for your account in a few minutes.

## The discount

| Plan | First year | From the second year |
| - | - | - |
| Pro yearly | \*\*$849.15** (15% off $999) | \$999 a year |
| Max yearly | \*\*$1,900** (5% off $2,000) | \$2,000 a year |

> [!NOTE]
>
> **The discount is on your first payment only.** Your plan renews at the full yearly price one year later: $999 for Pro, $2,000 for Max.

There is no non-profit discount on monthly plans, on Plus, or on the [Prompt Engineering Playbook](/agent-skills/prompt-engineering-playbook). Everything else about the plan is the same as the regular yearly plan: the same yearly tool-call pool and the same number of active ad accounts. See [Pricing](/knowledge-base/pricing) for what each plan includes.

## Who qualifies

* Your organisation is a **registered non-profit, NGO or charity**, in any country.
* **One discount per person and one per organisation.** Two people from the same organisation cannot each get one.
* Your current subscription does not already have another discount on it.

You confirm your organisation's status yourself. We record the details you give us with your discount.

> [!NOTE]
>
> **Already on Pro yearly or Max yearly?** The discount applies to the first payment of a new yearly plan. Ask in the support chat and a teammate will look at your account with you.

> [!TIP]
>
> **An agency, a large team, or many client accounts?** That is a different conversation. See [Enterprise](/knowledge-base/pricing) for plans sized to your accounts and team.

## What you need to send

When you ask for the discount, have these ready:

| Detail | Example |
| - | - |
| Your organisation's name | Save The Dogs Foundation |
| Its website | savethedogs.org |
| Its registration number | an EIN in the US, a charity number in the UK, a CNPJ in Brazil, or your country's equivalent |
| The country it is registered in | United States |
| One line, in your own words, confirming it is a registered non-profit | "We are a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit." |

## How to get your code

**1. Ask for it**

Sign in at [adsmcp.ai](https://adsmcp.si), open the support chat at the bottom-right, and say that your organisation is a non-profit. You can also email **[support@adsmcp.si](mailto:support@adsmcp.si)** from the address on your ADSMCP account.

**2. Confirm the price**

We tell you the exact price for the plan you want: the first year, and what it renews at. Say yes if it works for you.

**3. Send your organisation's details**

Send the five details above in one message.

**4. Get your code**

You receive a code that looks like `NONPROFIT-K7Q2ZP`, with the date it expires.

**5. Use it at checkout**

On the Home page at [adsmcp.ai](https://adsmcp.si), choose **Pro yearly** or **Max yearly** (the plan the code is for), and enter the code in the promotion code box on the checkout page. The order summary shows the discounted first-year price before you pay.

> [!WARNING]
>
> **Your code works only on the ADSMCP account it was issued to, only once, and only for 14 days.** It does not work on a monthly plan or on the other yearly plan. If you lose it, ask in the support chat and you get the same code again.

## FAQ

> **Can I get the non-profit discount on a monthly plan?**
>
> No. The discount is only on the first year of Pro yearly or Max yearly.

> **Does my plan renew at the discounted price?**
>
> No. The discount covers your first payment. From the second year your plan renews at the full yearly price: $999 for Pro, $2,000 for Max.

> **My code expired before I used it. What now?**
>
> Codes are valid for 14 days and there is one per person. Ask in the support chat or email [support@adsmcp.si](mailto:support@adsmcp.si), and a teammate will look at it with you.

> **Can a colleague use my code?**
>
> No. The code is locked to the account it was issued to, and the discount is one per organisation.

> **Can I combine it with another offer?**
>
> No. A subscription carries one discount at a time.
