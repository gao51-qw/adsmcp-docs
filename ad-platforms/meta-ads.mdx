ADSMCP connects your AI client to Meta Ads (Facebook and Instagram): ad accounts, campaigns, ad sets, ads, insights and breakdowns.

## Connect

Connect Meta on [Connections](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections) (OAuth). Then call `get_connections_status` and `get_ad_accounts`.

- Pass the ad account id (`act_...`) as a string.
- Budgets default to the account currency's **minor units** (`2500` = 25.00). Tools that accept `budget_unit: "major"` take normal units instead.

## What you can do

- **Report:** account, campaign and ad insights; creative, placement and demographic breakdowns; pixel event stats.
- **Analyze:** `audit_entire_account`, `diagnose_campaign_health`, `detect_creative_fatigue`, `analyze_meta_ad_performance`, `analyze_meta_audiences`, `optimize_meta_placements` (ranking only).
- **Plan:** targeting search and browse, promotable apps, Instagram accounts, products and existing assets.
- **Change (approval):** create campaigns, ad sets and ads (paused), or one-call image, video and carousel campaigns (traffic, awareness and engagement objectives; video needs an existing `video_id`); dynamic creatives, catalog ads, product sets, lead forms, offline conversion uploads and A/B test plans; pause / enable campaigns and ad sets; campaign budget changes; copies (always paused).

Not connected yet: listing lead forms and leads, custom audiences, ad creatives, permissions, product catalogs and sets, pixels, offline event sets and experiments. Those reads return `unavailable`.

## Changes need your approval

Every change below is staged as a pending action. Nothing changes in the account until a person approves it on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page; your AI client can view or reject it, never approve it. When an approved change runs, new objects are created paused or as drafts, budget changes and enables are checked against your [spend cap](/safety#spend-cap), and a failure is not retried. `remove_*` / `delete_*` tools also need `confirm_delete: true`.
