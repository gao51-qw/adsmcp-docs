ADSMCP is an MCP (Model Context Protocol) server and dashboard for paid media. Connect your ad accounts once, then ask the AI client you already use — Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor, Gemini CLI and others — to read performance, find waste, check drafts and propose changes.

- **Your model, our data.** ADSMCP supplies ad-account data, computed analysis and change proposals. The reasoning and writing happen in your own AI client with your own model; ADSMCP does not provide one.
- **A person approves every change.** Creates, pauses, enables, budget and bid changes are staged as pending actions. Only a person approves them, on the dashboard [Approvals](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/approvals) page. New campaigns are created paused, budget changes and enables are checked against your spend cap, and deletes need an explicit `confirm_delete`.

| [Google Ads](/ad-platforms/google-ads) | Accounts, campaigns, ad groups, ads, keywords and assets; performance reports and waste analysis; paused creation of Search, Performance Max, Display, YouTube, Demand Gen, Shopping and App campaigns. |
| [Meta Ads](/ad-platforms/meta-ads) | Accounts, campaigns, ad sets, ads, insights and breakdowns; audits and fatigue checks; paused campaign, ad set and ad creation. |

| [Google Analytics 4](/marketing-stack/google-analytics), [Klaviyo](/marketing-stack/klaviyo) | Reporting reads; one Klaviyo campaign draft. |

## How it fits together

1. You connect ad accounts on the [dashboard](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/connections).
2. Your AI client connects to `https://mcp.adsmcp.si/mcp` (OAuth or an API key).
3. The client calls ADSMCP tools. Reads return data right away. Changes come back as pending actions.
4. You approve or reject each pending action on the dashboard. Approved changes run, and you can follow them on the Execution Queue.

Plans start free (15 tool calls a month). See [Pricing](/pricing).
