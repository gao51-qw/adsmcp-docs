Plans and your current usage are on [Usage & Billing](https://adsmcp.si/dashboard/billing). `get_usage_status` returns your usage from any AI client.

## Monthly plans

| Plan | Price per month | Tool calls per month | Active ad accounts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | 15 | 1 per platform |
| Plus | $49 | 150 | 1 per platform |
| Pro | $99 | 600 | 1 per platform |
| Max | $199 | 3,000 | Up to 5 per platform |

## Annual billing

Annual plans cost the monthly price × 12 with 17% off: $488 for Plus, $986 for Pro and $1,982 for Max per year. The tool-call quota is the same monthly allowance.

## What a tool call is

One MCP tool call, or one `POST /api/v1/tools/{name}/execute`, counts as a call. Reading connections, pulling a report and validating a draft are calls. Staging a change is a call. Approving it on the dashboard is not.

A call over your allowance is refused with `quota_exceeded`. There are no overage charges.

## AI model costs

ADSMCP does not include or resell an AI model. Your AI client (Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, …) runs on your own subscription or API key. Dashboard features that write text use the provider key you add in [Settings](/settings).

## Payments and refunds

Payments are processed by Paddle. New paid subscriptions can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase; see the [refund policy](https://adsmcp.si/refund).

Billing questions: [sales@adsmcp.si](mailto:sales@adsmcp.si).

## Self-hosting

Running ADSMCP on your own infrastructure keeps the database, ad-account grants and audit log there. The approval, spend-cap and delete rules still apply.
